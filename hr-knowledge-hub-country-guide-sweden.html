Salary Benchmark

Wage system — no statutory minimum wage

  • Sweden has no statutory minimum wage; wages are set by 500+ collective agreements (Kollektivavtal)
  • CBA minimum monthly salaries are approx. SEK 22,000 – 25,000 (depending on industry / experience) and cover about 90% of the workforce
  • In 2026, around 500 collective agreements are due for renegotiation, affecting more than 3.4 million employees

Total additional employment cost (incl. social security + holiday pay + occupational pension + insurance)

Approx. 40 – 50% of employee gross salary (incl. social security 31.42% + holiday pay supplement + CBA pension ~4–5% + insurance ~1%).

Mandatory / Common Benefits

  • Sick leave day 1: qualifying deduction day (Karensavdrag · deduction of 20% of average weekly wage); days 2–14 paid by employer at 80%
  • From sick leave day 15: Försäkringskassan pays about 80% (cap SEK 1,200 / day)
  • Occupational pension (Tjänstepension): set by CBA (usually 4–5% of salary); plus 24.26% special payroll tax
  • Holiday pay supplement (Semestertillägg): additional 0.43% of monthly salary per day, or 12% of variable wage

Overtime Pay

  • Overtime pay is set by CBA or employment contract (no statutory uniform multiplier)
  • Common CBA standard: weekday overtime +50–68%; weekend / public holiday +100%
  • Annual overtime cap: 200 hours; can be extended to 350 hours in special circumstances

Key Regulations

Employment Protection Act LAS (2022 reform)

Major reform in 2022; dismissals no longer need to follow seniority order; small companies (< 50 employees) may exempt 3 people; no salary payment is required during disputes.

Collective Agreement System (Kollektivavtal)

Around 500+ agreements cover about 90% of the workforce; they set wages, working hours, pensions, insurance, etc.; many agreements expire and will be renegotiated in 2026.

480 Days of Parental Leave (one of the world's most generous)

Parents share 480 days; 390 days are paid at approx. 80% salary (capped); each parent must use at least 90 non-transferable days.

Foreign Expert Tax Regime (Expertskatt)

High-skilled foreign employees receive a 25% income tax exemption for the first 5 years; salary threshold must be met (approx. SEK 103,500 / month).

EU Pay Transparency Directive (implementation in summer 2026)

Employers must disclose pay ranges and gender pay gap data; starting salary ranges must be provided during recruitment; affects all employers.
