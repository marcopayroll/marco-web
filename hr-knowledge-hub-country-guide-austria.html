Salary Benchmark

No statutory minimum wage · set by 800+ industry CBAs

  • Approx. 95–98% of private-sector employees are covered by CBA (Kollektivvertrag).
  • Most CBAs set minimum monthly wages at €1,700+ (14-month salary system) · average around €1,983.
  • National average gross monthly salary is around €3,800 – 4,000 (14-salary basis · around USD 4,200).

Mandatory / Common Benefits

  • 13th + 14th salary (holiday + Christmas bonus): paid in June + November, 1 month salary each · mandatory under CBA
  • Preferential tax rate for double salary: bonuses are taxed at a low 6% rate (far lower than progressive wage tax)
  • New severance system (Abfertigung Neu): employer contributes 1.53% to the employee account (portable between jobs)
  • Public transport subsidy: employer may provide an annual pass (fully tax-free) · promoted since 2021

Overtime Pay (Überstunden)

  • Basic overtime: +50% (1.5×) · or equivalent time off (1.5× per day)
  • Night / holiday overtime: +100% (depending on CBA)
  • From 2026: overtime within 10 hours per month is tax-free (up to €120)
  • All-in contracts require an annual “coverage check” (Deckungsprüfung) · excess must be paid separately

Dismissal Protection and Severance

  • White-collar notice period: employer 6 weeks to 5 months (depending on seniority) · employee 1 month
  • Severance pay (new system Abfertigung Neu · since 2003): employer contributes 1.53% to the employee account
  • Unfair dismissal may trigger special social protection (e.g. pregnancy / disability / works council representative)

Key Regulations

Collective Agreement System (Kollektivverträge · CBA)

800+ industry CBAs cover 95–98% of private-sector employees; replace a statutory minimum wage; negotiated by WKO + ÖGB.

14-Salary System + 6% Preferential Bonus Tax Rate

13th + 14th salary (holiday + Christmas bonus); enjoy low 6% tax rate; distinctive system in Austria / Germany.

New Severance Pay System (Abfertigung Neu)

2003 reform; employer contributes 1.53% to the employee personal account (BV); portable between jobs.

EU Pay Transparency Directive (effective 2026/6)

Austria will implement the EU Pay Transparency Directive; affects pay structures + disclosure requirements.

Anti-Wage and Social Dumping Act (LSD-BG)

Strictly enforces CBA minimum wages; heavy penalties for violations; applies equally to posted / outsourced workers.
