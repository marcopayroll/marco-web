Salary Benchmark

Singapore has no unified minimum wage. Only Cleaning / Security / Landscaping / Elevator / Food services etc. industries apply PWM (Progressive Wage Model)

  • Cleaning worker PWM starting salary S$ 1,740 / Month (2026), Security S$ 3,530 / Month (Senior Security Officer)
  • AWS 13th Month Salary not mandatory, most employers pay by contract / company policy, performance bonus 1-3 months salary common
  • 2025 full-time median monthly salary S$ 5,500 (Gross, MOM), including CPF, compared to 2024 increase of 3.5%

Mandatory Benefits / Common Benefits

  • AWS (Year-end Bonus / 13th Month Salary): Not mandatory, most employers provide, approximately 1/13 of annual salary (7.7%)
  • Medical Insurance (Group H&S): Most employers provide, covering outpatient / hospitalization / dental, monthly S$ 80-200 / person
  • Life / Accident Insurance: Medium and large employer standard, usually 24-36 months salary coverage, GTL + GPA
  • Flexible Benefits (Flexi Benefits): Gym / Training / Commuting, annual budget S$ 500-2,000

Overtime (Overtime), Employment Act Part IV

  • OT 1.5× hourly rate (Workers monthly salary ≤ S$4,500, Non-workers monthly salary ≤ S$2,600), mandatory minimum
  • Rest day work: Within half day 1× day wages, beyond half day 2× day wages, employer requires 1.5×, employee requests 0.5×
  • Public holiday work: 1 day additional wages (1×) + optional OT 1.5×, total 2.5× equivalent
  • White-collar / High-salary employees (Non-Part IV): Entirely by contract / company policy agreement, most monthly salary systems do not calculate separately

Employment Act (1968) applicable to all employees (Including foreign / senior), Part IV (Working hours / OT / Rest) only applicable to lower-paid workers / non-workers

Dismissal Protection and Compensation

  • Notice period (Notice): 1 day (< 26 weeks) → 4 weeks (≥ 5 years), contract may set longer, mutually applicable
  • Retrenchment compensation (Retrenchment): No unified statutory requirement, NTUC guideline approximately 2 weeks - 1 month / year of service (Service completing 2 years)
  • Summary dismissal (Without notice): Only for serious misconduct (Theft / Fraud / Absenteeism / Insubordination), must be substantiated
  • Unfair dismissal: MOM TADM mediation → ECT Employment Claims Tribunal, service ≥ 6 months may apply for reinstatement / compensation

Key Regulations

Employment Act (EA, 1968) - Labor Law Main Body

Singapore labor law core, 2019 amendment expanded coverage to all employees (Excluding seafarers / civil servants), working hours / OT protection only applicable to Part IV scope. MOM oversight

CPF Act - Central Provident Fund Act

Local citizens / PR mandatory, three accounts (OA Ordinary + SA Special + MA Medical), 2026/1 monthly salary cap raised to S$ 8,000, foreign employees (EP) exempt

PWM Progressive Wage Model - Progressive Wage Model

Cleaning / Security / Landscaping / Elevator / Retail / F&B etc. industries applicable, graduated wages + training pathway, replacing national minimum wage

WSHA Workplace Safety & Health Act - Workplace Safety Act

Employer must ensure workplace safety, high-risk industries (Construction / Manufacturing) mandatory safety training, violation penalty up to S$ 500,000 + imprisonment 2 years

PDPA Personal Data Protection Act - Personal Data Protection Act

2012 enacted, 2021 amended (Breach notification + Data portability), PDPC enforcement, penalty cap S$ 1 million or annual revenue 10%

WPF Workplace Fairness Act 2026 - Fair Employment Act (New Legislation)

2026 effective, prohibiting age / gender / race / religion / marital status / disability / pregnancy etc. 7 types of discrimination. TADM handles complaints
