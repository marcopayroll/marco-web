Salary Benchmark

Statutory Minimum Wage SNMG (from January 2026)

  • SNMG: DZD 24,000 / month (+20%) · approx. USD 175 · first adjustment since 2020
  • National average gross monthly salary approx. DZD 43,000 – 45,000 (approx. USD 317)
  • Currency: DZD (dinar) · strict foreign exchange controls · large gap between informal and official exchange rates

Total Additional Employer Cost

Including CNAS 26% + training 1% + apprenticeship 1%: approx. 28 – 30% of the employee gross salary (excluding optional 13th salary / year-end bonus)

Mandatory Benefits / Common Benefits

  • Sick leave: CNAS pays 50% from the 16th day (later increasing to 100%); employer pays the first 15 days (according to CBA)
  • Maternity leave: 150 days at full pay (paid by CNAS · may be extended by 50–165 days for a newborn with disability)
  • Annual leave: 30 calendar days / year (statutory) · additional days increase with length of service
  • 13th salary / year-end bonus: not statutory mandatory, but common market practice (paid around Eid al-Fitr / year-end)

Overtime Pay

  • First 8 overtime hours: +50% of basic hourly wage
  • Overtime exceeding 8 hours: +100% of basic hourly wage
  • Public holiday work: +200–300% of basic hourly wage (according to collective agreement)

Contracts must be in writing (Arabic or French); registration with CNAS is required within 10 days of onboarding.

Dismissal Protection and Compensation

  • Economic redundancy requires approval from the Labour Inspectorate; consultation with the union must be conducted in advance.
  • Notice period: according to the collective agreement (usually 1–3 months).
  • Severance pay: at least 15 days of wages for each completed year of service (may be increased by CBA).

Key Regulations

Labour Law (Loi 90-11 · 1990)

Core Algerian labour law; regulates contracts, working hours, leave, dismissal protection, collective bargaining, and other matters.

CNAS Social Security System

Employer 26% + employee 9% (one of the highest in Africa / the Middle East); covers medical care / pension / unemployment / work injury.

2026 Minimum Wage Increased by 20%

SNMG increased from DZD 20,000 to DZD 24,000 (first increase since 2020); reflects inflation pressure.

Foreign Worker Employment Restrictions

A work permit from the Ministry of Labour is required; priority is given to Algerian nationals; the oil and gas industry has special approval procedures.

Foreign Exchange Controls

Wages must be paid in DZD; foreign employees may remit part of their income abroad subject to approval; there is a large gap between official and informal exchange rates.
