## Structure
Pages are grouped by filename prefix:
- `index.html` — Homepage
- `company-*` — About, contact, legal
- `service-*` — Service pages
- `compliance-*` — Compliance pages
- `price-*` — Pricing & proposal builder
- `hr-knowledge-hub-*` — Country/visa/payroll guides, memos, tools
- `marco-os.html` — Marco OS product page
