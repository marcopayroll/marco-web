Scope & Application

Applicable Scope and Legal Basis / LFT · LSS · LISR · SAT · IMSS · INFONAVIT

This document applies to:

Companies that have established a local legal entity in Mexico (S.A. de C.V. / S. de R.L. de C.V. / S.A.P.I.; minimum registered capital as low as MXN 1)
Employees directly employed by the local entity, subject to the Ley Federal del Trabajo (LFT) and the “true employer” rules (Outsourcing 2021 Reform)
Payroll compliance based on the LFT, LSS (Social Security Law), LISR (Income Tax Law), Código Fiscal de la Federación, and regulations from SAT / IMSS / INFONAVIT

This framework explains how companies complete Payroll Set-up in Mexico (SAT tax registration RFC + IMSS employer registration + INFONAVIT + state ISN), monthly payroll calculation (ISR + IMSS + INFONAVIT 5% + 13 días Aguinaldo + Vacaciones + PTU), monthly filing and payment (ISR by the 17th / IMSS by the 17th / INFONAVIT bi-monthly), and annual compliance management (annual tax filing by April 30, PTU distribution by May 30).

MARCO INSIGHT

Three key characteristics of Mexico’s Payroll: (1) Outsourcing 2021 Reform: personal subcontracting-style staff leasing is prohibited (only specialized services with REPSE registration are allowed for outsourcing); all employees must be directly employed by the true employer. (2) UMA / Minimum Wage dual-track system: since 2016, IMSS / INFONAVIT / tax caps use UMA (decoupled from the minimum wage), while employee entitlements (such as severance pay) are still based on the minimum wage. (3) Mandatory statutory benefits are extremely complex: Aguinaldo 15 days + PTU 10% of profits + Vacaciones upgrade (2023 LFT reform: 12 days in the first year, increasing annually up to 32 days) + Prima Vacacional 25%.

MARCO INSIGHT

Three commonly underestimated details in Mexico Payroll: (1) Outsourcing / REPSE restrictions: since 2021, staff leasing-style outsourcing is prohibited; only SPECIALIZED SERVICES through REPSE registration are allowed. Violations result in classification as “direct employment” with full IMSS back-payment for all employees. (2) SBC integration is not just the monthly salary; it must also include Aguinaldo 15/365 + Vacaciones × 25% Prima converted to daily value. Most companies underestimate IMSS contributions by 6–8%. (3) PTU (Reparto de Utilidades) since the 2021 reform: the cap = 3 months’ salary of the employee OR the 5-year average PTU, whichever is higher. Employees cannot trade salary for PTU.

MARCO INSIGHT

Three key changes for Mexico Payroll in 2026: (1) Minimum wage +13%: general MXN 315.04/day (annual salary approximately MXN 115,000) / Northern Border Free Zone MXN 440.87/day; this cascading increase raises Aguinaldo, Indemnización, Prima, and other entitlements. (2) UMA increased to MXN 113.14/day, with IMSS SBC monthly cap at approximately MXN 86,066. (3) The 40-hour workweek reform is under legislative review (currently 48 hours) and is expected to progress throughout 2026; employers must recalculate overtime pay and shift arrangements.
