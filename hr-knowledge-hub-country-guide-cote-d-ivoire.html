Salary Benchmark

One of the Francophone countries (UEMOA), CFA Franc pegged to Euro at exchange rate 655.957, Abidjan is the economic center of West Africa

  • SMIG (General Workers) FCFA 75,000 / Month (2023/1 adjusted, 2026 maintained), SMAG (Agriculture) FCFA 60,000
  • National average monthly salary FCFA 178,000 (Gross, 2025 INS), median FCFA 140,000, Abidjan is significantly higher than the national average
  • 13 Salary / Year-End Bonus (Gratification): Industry Convention (CCI) mandated, Banking / Telecom / Petroleum commonly 1-2 months

Employer Additional Cost Summary (Local Employees, as % of Base Salary)

CNPS 12.7% (Retirement 7.7 + Family Allowance 5), CMU 3%, FDFP 1.2%, Work Injury ~2%, ITS-emp 2.8%

Total employer additional cost is approximately 18 - 22% of base salary, plus 13 Salary (Gratification Convention mandated), total cost additional +8%

Mandatory Benefits / Common Benefits

  • Prime de Transport Transportation Allowance: Statutory minimum FCFA 30,000 / Month (Abidjan area), can be higher per convention
  • Indemnité de Logement Housing Allowance: Not mandatory, but commonly provided by medium and large enterprises (25% of base salary)
  • 13 Salary (Gratification): Sector Convention (CCI) mandated, Banking / Petroleum / Telecom commonly 1-2 months salary
  • Medical Supplement (Mutuelle / GRAS Savoye / SUNU): Beyond CMU basic coverage, commonly provided by medium and large enterprises

Overtime (Heures Supplémentaires), CCI 1977

  • Weekly hours 41 to 46 = Hourly rate × 1.15
  • Weekly hours 47 and above = Hourly rate × 1.50, cumulative annual cap 75 hours / year
  • Sundays / Public holiday work = Hourly rate × 1.75, cumulative with OT
  • Senior / Management positions (Cadres au forfait): Usually fixed salary contract, OT not calculated separately

Code du Travail 2015: CDD renewed (≥ 2 times) is treated as CDI. Abuse of temporary contracts may result in labor bureau penalties.

Termination Protection and Compensation

  • Termination must have legal cause: economic reasons / incompetence / violation, must first warn (Avertissement) → hearing process
  • Notice period (Préavis): Workers 8 days, Staff 1 month, Managers 3 months (by job level / seniority increment)
  • Severance Allowance (Indemnité de Licenciement): 30% monthly salary / year (1-5 years), 35% (6-10 years), 40% (>10 years)
  • Wrongful Dismissal (Sans Cause Réelle): 6 - 18 months salary compensation, Labor Court (Tribunal du Travail) ruling

Key Regulations

Code du Travail (Loi 2015-532) — Core Labor Law

Core labor law of Côte d’Ivoire, covering working hours / wages / termination / leave / unions, enforced by Ministère du Travail and Inspection du Travail

Convention Collective Interprofessionnelle (CCI 1977) — Cross-Industry Collective Convention

Cross-industry minimum standards for external laws, 11 occupational category salary baselines (Classes 1-11), 13 Salary / Allowances / Termination / Training / Working hours specifics

CMU (Couverture Maladie Universelle, 2019) — Universal Health Insurance

Legislation 2014-131 implemented, 2019 mandatory, replacing AMU. Employee 1.5% + Employer 3%, CNPS administered, covering RBM basic medical care

Réforme ITS 2022 — Payroll Tax Reform

2022/1 onwards combined ITS + IGR + CN + IGR cédulaire into unified ITS, 4-tier progressive (0/12/20/25%), simplified employer operations

Loi Mobile Money / Digital Payroll — Digital Salary Payment

From 2023 encouraged salary payment via MTN / Orange / Wave Money, BCEAO regulated, anti-money laundering, encouraging financial inclusion

CGRAE / RGCE — Civil Servant Pension Fund (Public Sector)

CNPS only applies to private sector. Civil servants / State-owned enterprises separately managed by CGRAE / IPS-CGRAE. Employee 8.33% + State 17.55%
