Salary Benchmark

Statutory minimum wage (from January 2025)

  • Minimum monthly wage (RMV): PEN 1,130 (+10%) · approx. USD 305 · nationwide.
  • National average gross monthly salary is approx. PEN 2,600 – 2,900 (early 2026 estimate).
  • 48-hour working week (6 days x 8 hours · Latin America standard).

Direct employer social security is only 9% (EsSalud), but with Gratificaciones + CTS included, total cost is approx. 35–40%.

Annualized total employer cost (including EsSalud + Gratificaciones + CTS + vacation pay)

Approx. 135–140% of the employee monthly salary. Annualized total cost is around 14.2–16.8 months of salary, a relatively high level in Latin America.

Other mandatory benefits

  • Family allowance: employees with minor children receive an additional 10% of the minimum wage (PEN 113 / month).
  • Life insurance (Seguro Vida Ley): employer must insure employees after 4 years of service; some companies provide it from onboarding.
  • SCTR high-risk industry insurance: mining, construction and other high-risk sectors must purchase additional work injury / occupational disease insurance.

Overtime Pay (Horas extras)

  • First 2 overtime hours: +25% of hourly wage.
  • From the 3rd overtime hour onward: +35% of hourly wage.
  • Overtime must be voluntary and cannot be forced; written records are required.
  • Night-shift allowance: at least 35% above the minimum wage.

Part-time (Tiempo parcial)—Less than 4 hours per day; no CTS / Gratificaciones
Outsourcing / service contract—Must operate independently; not an employment relationship

Contracts must be written in Spanish. Indefinite-term employment is the default. During probation, termination may be made without cause.

Dismissal protection and compensation

  • Dismissal must have a valid legal cause. Termination without cause is considered unfair dismissal.
  • Unfair dismissal compensation: 1.5 months of salary per completed year of service, capped at 12 months of salary.
  • CTS severance savings are paid in full on termination, plus compensation for unused vacation and prorated unpaid Gratificaciones.

Key Regulations

Labor Productivity and Competitiveness Law (DL 728 / DS 003-97-TR)

Core Peruvian labour law. Regulates employment contracts, dismissal, benefits, overtime and related matters, supported by multiple implementing regulations.

Gratificaciones double-bonus system

One month salary is paid in July and one month in December, plus a 9% special bonus (Bonificación Extraordinaria). This is a distinctive Latin American benefit system.

CTS severance savings system

Every six months, the employer deposits approx. half a month of salary into the employee's bank account. The full balance is withdrawn upon termination; it functions like mandatory savings.

Profit sharing (Participación en Utilidades)

Mandatory for enterprises with 100+ employees. Depending on the industry, 5–10% of pre-tax profit is distributed to employees.

Foreign employee hiring limits

Foreign employees may not exceed 20% of the total workforce or 30% of total payroll. A work authorization from the Ministry of Labour is required.
