Salary Benchmark

UAE has no unified statutory minimum wage for foreign employees, visa category sets effective salary thresholds

  • Local national private sector employees minimum monthly salary AED 6,000 (Effective 2026/1, MOHRE mandatory)
  • Work visa minimum salary thresholds: Approx. AED 4,000 (Low-skilled) to AED 20,000+ (Senior white-collar)
  • UAE national average monthly salary approx. AED 15,000 (Approx. USD 4,080), Dubai private sector significantly higher than national average

Mandatory Benefits / Common Benefits

  • WPS Wage Protection System: All wages must be paid via MOHRE approved electronic channels, delays trigger penalties
  • Health Insurance: Dubai and Abu Dhabi mandatory for all employees, monthly salary < AED 4,000 must provide Essential Benefits Plan
  • End-of-Service Gratuity: Entitled from 1 year of service, first 5 years at 21 days / year, after 5 years at 30 days / year, cap 2 years salary
  • 13th Month Salary / Bonus: Not mandatory, common in banking / finance / large multinationals, can be agreed through contract

Overtime (Overtime)

  • Regular overtime = Normal hourly rate × 125%, monthly overtime should not exceed 144 hours
  • Night / Holiday / Rest day overtime = Normal hourly rate × 150%, or compensatory rest + 50%
  • Senior management / Security / Domestic workers not subject to overtime rules, must be stated in contract
  • All-in / Lump-sum contracts common in finance and technology industries, must specify overtime coverage in contract

Dismissal Protection and Compensation

  • Probation period maximum 6 months, during probation either party may terminate with 14 days written notice
  • Standard dismissal: Advance 30 - 90 days written notice, unlawful dismissal may be adjudicated 3 months salary compensation
  • End-of-Service Gratuity: Entitled from 1 year, first 5 years at 21 days / year, after 5 years at 30 days / year, cap 2 years salary
  • Unemployment Insurance ILOE (2023 Mandatory): Maximum 3 months post-unemployment, monthly salary cap AED 20,000

Key Regulations

Federal Decree-Law 33/2021 - Modern Labor Law Main Body

Effective 2022/2; abolished indefinite term contracts; unified to fixed-term (Maximum 3 years); explicit anti-discrimination, anti-harassment, equal pay

Emiratisation Nationalization Quota - Private Enterprise Core Compliance

2026 target: 50+ employee enterprises must have UAE national employee ratio of 10%; non-compliance penalty AED 96,000 / person / year, assessed semi-annually

DEWS Savings Plan (DIFC Exclusive) - Replacing Traditional Gratuity

DIFC free zone from 2020 mandatory; employer contributes 5.83% / 8.33% monthly basic salary to custodian account; employees may choose own investment portfolio

Green Visa / Golden Visa - Independent Work Visa

Green Visa (5 years) for freelancers / skilled workers; Golden Visa (10 years) for investors / specialists / outstanding talent; holders may self-sponsor without employer

Wage Protection System (WPS) - Mandatory Electronic Wage Payment

All private sector employers must pay wages via MOHRE approved bank / exchange house / e-wallet; delays / underpayment triggers visa freeze and penalties

UAE Corporate Tax (CT) - Effective 2023/6

9% rate applicable to taxable profit > AED 375,000; free zone qualifying enterprises still enjoy 0%; employment wage income not subject to CT
