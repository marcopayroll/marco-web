Salary Benchmark

Statutory Minimum Wage Asgari Ücret (Effective January 1, 2026)

  • Gross wages (Brüt): TL 33,030 / Month, Net wages (Net): TL 28,075 / Month, Daily: TL 1,101
  • 2026 increase +27% (2025 TL 26,005 → 2026 TL 33,030); covers all industries nationwide
  • Over 1/3 of workforce receives minimum wage; minimum wage exempt from personal income tax and stamp duty (Since 2022)

Mandatory Benefits

  • Severance Pay (Kidem Tazminati): Per completed 1 year of service = 30 days gross wages; 2026 H1 cap TL 35,058.58
  • Notice Pay (Ihbar Tazminati): When terminated without required notice period, must pay corresponding notice period wages
  • Private Pension Auto-enrollment (OKS / BES): Employees auto-enrolled, 3% salary contribution; employees may opt out within 2 months
  • Bi-annual Bonus (Customary, not statutory): Most employers provide year-end bonus and religious holiday bonuses

Personal Income Tax (Gelir Vergisi)

  • Progressive system (Artan Oranli): Annual cumulative taxable income taxed by tier; employer monthly withholding
  • Minimum wage fully exempt from personal income tax and stamp duty (Since 2022)

Key Regulations

Labor Law No. 4857 (Is Kanunu)

2003 enacted, covers majority of private sector employees; regulates contracts, working hours, leave, dismissal protection and other core labor relations

Social Insurance Law No. 5510

Unified pension / healthcare / work injury / unemployment insurance system; employer must register with SGK 1 day before employee start date

Foreign Worker Work Permit

Must apply through labor department for Çalisma Izni; salary generally must reach 1.5× minimum wage (Some industries 2-4×)

Occupational Health and Safety Law No. 6331

Applicable to all enterprises; > 50 employees must appoint occupational health doctor and safety specialist; periodic health checks / risk assessment mandatory

Data Protection Law KVKK No. 6698

Similar to GDPR; employee personal data processing must comply; cross-border transfers require approval

Unions and Collective Bargaining

Union membership freedom protected by constitution; collective agreements (TIS) may stipulate benefits higher than statutory standards
