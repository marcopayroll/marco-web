Salary Benchmark

Malaysia has a nationwide minimum wage of RM 1,700. Actual median pay in Kuala Lumpur / Selangor is significantly above the national average.

  • National minimum wage: RM 1,700 / month (effective from Feb 2025, continuing in 2026, covers all enterprises).
  • National monthly median salary: RM 3,036; average salary: RM 3,441 (2025 Q2 DOSM formal-sector data).
  • Fresh university graduates (bachelor degree) median: RM 3,085; Kuala Lumpur approx. RM 3,435.

Foreign employees are exempt from mandatory EPF (may contribute voluntarily) and must join SOCSO employment-injury coverage. Income tax depends on resident / non-resident status.

Total employer add-on cost (local employees — as % of base salary)

EPF 12–13% · SOCSO 1.75% · EIS 0.2% · HRDF 1% (where applicable). Total employer add-on cost is approx. 15–17% (≤ RM 5K) / 14–16% (> RM 5K), plus medical benefits and bonuses.

Mandatory / common benefits

  • EPF / KWSP: one-stop retirement protection; may be used for first-home down payment, major illness and children's education.
  • SOCSO / PERKESO: employment injury, occupational disease, invalidity and survivors' protection; salary cap RM 6,000.
  • Medical insurance: not mandatory, but commonly provided by medium and large employers (GHS / GTL / outpatient) as a core benefit.
  • 13th-month salary / bonus: not mandatory; common in finance, petrochemical and multinational companies; private-sector average 1–3 months.

Overtime

  • Weekday overtime = normal hourly rate x 150%; applies to hours beyond 8 per day.
  • Rest-day overtime = 50% of half-day pay or 100% of full-day pay; hours beyond normal working hours are paid at 200%.
  • Public-holiday overtime = 200% hourly rate + replacement rest; hours beyond normal working hours are paid at 300%.
  • OT rules apply only to EA employees earning ≤ RM 4,000 per month; higher-income employees follow contract terms.

From Jan 2023, the Employment Act 1955 expanded coverage to all monthly-paid employees (previous RM 2,000 cap removed). Part XII rules on working hours / OT still apply only to employees earning ≤ RM 4,000 per month.

Dismissal protection and termination payments

  • Probation is usually 3–6 months. Dismissal during probation still requires a valid reason; termination without notice may be treated as unfair.
  • Typical notice period: service <2 years = 4 weeks; 2–5 years = 6 weeks; >5 years = 8 weeks, unless the contract provides otherwise.
  • Termination / gratuity compensation: approx. 10 days per year (<2 years), 15 days per year (2–5 years), 20 days per year (>5 years).
  • EIS unemployment insurance: up to 6 months of benefits after job loss, based on contribution record, around 30–80% of salary.

Key Regulations

Employment Act 1955 (2022 amendment) — core modern labour law

Effective Jan 2023. Covers all monthly-paid employees (previous RM 2,000 cap removed), reduces weekly hours to 45, increases maternity leave to 98 days and introduces 7 days of paternity leave.

E-Invoicing mandatory compliance — LHDN

Phased rollout began in Aug 2024. From Jul 2026, all businesses with revenue of RM 150,000+ must issue e-Invoices through the MyInvois portal / API.

EPF i-Saraan / i-Suri — voluntary retirement savings schemes

Self-employed individuals and housewives may voluntarily contribute to EPF / KWSP. Government matching subsidy of 15% is available, capped at RM 500 per year, with the same retirement benefits.

HRD Corp Levy — human resources development training fund

Mandatory for employers with 10+ Malaysian employees in specified industries. Monthly levy is 1% of wages; 85 specified industries; employers may claim reimbursement for employee training costs.

Foreign Worker Levy — foreign worker levy (PLKS)

Employers pay RM 410–1,850 per year for foreign blue-collar workers, graded by industry (manufacturing / construction / plantation / services). Employer bears the cost and may not deduct it from wages.

SST — Sales and Service Tax expansion in 2025

Sales tax 5–10%; service tax 6–8% (increased from 6% to 8% from Mar 2024, except food & beverage and parking). Expanded to 7 new service categories from Jul 2025.
