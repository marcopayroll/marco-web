Total additional employer cost (including ZUS + PPK + leave accrual)

Approx. 21–25% of employee gross salary (including ZUS ~20% + PPK 1.5% + leave and sick leave accruals).

Mandatory / common benefits

  • PPK employee capital plan: employer basic contribution 1.5% + employee 2% (each can add up to 4%); default enrolment with opt-out.
  • First 33 days of sick leave: employer pays 80% (first 14 days for employees aged 50+); ZUS pays 80% thereafter.
  • Social Fund (ZFŚS): companies with 20+ employees must establish it; supports holidays, culture, children's activities, etc.

Personal Income Tax

Income Tax (PIT)

Two progressive brackets: 12% / 32%; employer withholds monthly advances; tax-free amount PLN 30,000 / year.

  • Youth tax relief under 26 (Zerowy PIT): annual income up to PLN 85,528 is exempt from PIT.
  • Families with 4+ children: each parent may use a PLN 85,528 tax exemption.
  • Returning expatriates / people moving to Poland: partial PIT exemption for the first 4 years (PIT-0).

Dismissal protection and severance

  • Termination of an indefinite-term contract requires a valid reason and written notice.
  • Notice period: 2 weeks (< 6 months) · 1 month (6 months – 3 years) · 3 months (3 years+).
  • Collective redundancy severance: 1 month (< 2 years) · 2 months (2–8 years) · 3 months (8+ years).

Key Regulations

Labour Code (Kodeks pracy)

Enacted in 1974 and amended multiple times (including 2023 remote-work rules and 2026 civil-contract reclassification). It is Poland's core employment law.

PPK employee capital plan (from 2019)

Employers are required to establish PPK; employees are enrolled by default (with opt-out). The state provides PLN 250 annually plus a one-off PLN 250 welcome payment.

2023 remote-work law amendment

Remote work has been incorporated into the Labour Code; employers must cover equipment and costs, and hybrid work is permitted.

Youth tax relief under 26 (Zerowy PIT)

Annual income up to PLN 85,528 is exempt from PIT; applies to employment contracts and mandate contracts.

EU member state + foreigner work permits

EU / EEA citizens may work freely; Ukrainian citizens follow simplified procedures; other third-country nationals need a work permit + residence permit.
