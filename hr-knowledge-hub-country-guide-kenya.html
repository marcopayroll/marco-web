Salary Benchmark

Kenya divides cities into 3 minimum wage tiers (Tier 1 – 3), additionally 17 occupation-specific differentials, 2026 temporarily not adjusted upward

  • Nairobi / Mombasa / Kisumu / Nakuru (Tier 1): General worker KES 16,114 / month (2024/11 onwards)
  • Domestic worker / Security / Driver / Other industries set separate minimum wages, must pay 15% housing allowance (If housing not provided)
  • National average monthly salary KES 71,200 (Gross, 2025 KNBS), median approximately KES 50,000, Nairobi leading by 30%

Employer Total Additional Cost (Local Employee, as % of Basic Salary)

NSSF 6%, AHL 1.5%, WIBA ~1%, NITA fixed amount, no SHIF employer portion. Employer total additional cost approximately 10 – 12% of basic salary (UEL threshold), above KES 72K the ratio decreases

Mandatory Benefits / Common Benefits

  • Housing Allowance (House Allowance): Employment Act § 31 mandatory, usually 15% of basic salary
  • WIBA Work Injury Insurance: Mandatory employer purchase from private insurance company, covering work injury / occupational disease / death
  • Medical Supplement: Medium and large enterprises commonly provide (AAR / Jubilee / Britam), beyond SHIF basic coverage
  • 13th Month Salary / Year-End Bonus: Not statutory, banking / telecom / manufacturing commonly provide, 1 – 2 months salary varies

Overtime, Employment Act § 28

  • Workday overtime = Regular hourly wage × 1.5, applicable when daily exceeds 8 hours
  • Rest day / Public holiday overtime = Regular hourly wage × 2, double wages
  • Senior / Managerial positions (Senior / Managerial): Usually contracted as all-inclusive package, overtime not separately calculated
  • Night shift (22:00 – 06:00): Some industries per CBA set +25% night shift differential

Casual contracts continuing 24 days automatically convert to Permanent (EA § 37); abuse of short-term contracts treated as long-term employment

Dismissal Protection and Compensation

  • Lawful grounds (EA § 41-45): Serious misconduct / Business restructuring / Incompetence / Retirement, must submit written application and attend hearing procedure
  • Notice period: Shorter of 1 month salary or 28 days, both parties 1 month, notice must be paid in lieu
  • Severance pay (Redundancy): Minimum 15 days salary / year of service, plus 1 month salary in lieu of notice, plus unused annual leave cash-out
  • Unfair Dismissal: Industrial Court may order maximum 12 months salary compensation, no reinstatement required with additional compensation

Key Regulations

Employment Act 2007 – Labor Law Main Body

Kenya labor law core, covering working hours / wages / dismissal / leave / anti-discrimination, enforced by Ministry of Labour and Employment Court jointly

Social Health Insurance Act 2024 (SHIA) – NHIF → SHIF Reform

From 2024/10 SHIF replacing NHIF, employee pays 2.75% gross salary (Minimum KES 300), three funds: PHC / SHIF / ECCIF, mandatory universal

Affordable Housing Act 2024 (AHL) – Affordable Housing Levy

From 2023/3 mandatory, employer + employee each 1.5% gross salary, 2024/12 onwards cancelling employee personal tax deduction, used to supply affordable housing

NSSF Act 2013 – Pension Reform (5-Year Phased Implementation)

Replacing old 5% fixed amount, 2024 onwards 6% bilateral matching, UEL increasing annually, 2026 Year 3 (UEL 72K), 2027 Year 5 (UEL standard)

Data Protection Act 2019 – GDPR Equivalent

Employer collecting employee data must clearly inform + consent + limited use, enforced by ODPC Data Protection Bureau, violators fined maximum KES 5 million / 1% revenue

Finance Act 2025/2026 – Annual Tax Adjustments

AHL exemption cancelled (2024/12), digital services tax 1.5%, digital asset tax 3%, iTax system electronic filing, KRA strict audits
