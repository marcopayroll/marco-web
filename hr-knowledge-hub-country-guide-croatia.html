Salary Benchmark

Statutory minimum wage (from January 2026)

  • Minimum gross wage: €1,050 / month (+8.2%); cumulative increase of 153% since 2016.
  • Minimum gross wage for directors / management: €1,295.45 / month (coefficient 0.65 × average gross wage).
  • Croatia joined the Eurozone in January 2023; wages are denominated in EUR.

  • Employer pays 16.5% health insurance only; employee pays 20% pension insurance (15% first pillar + 5% second pillar); pension contribution cap €11,956 / month.
  • Low-income relief: for gross wages below €700, the base is reduced by €300; for €700 – 1,300, the reduction decreases by formula.

Total employer add-on cost (Gross II = gross wage + 16.5%)

Example: minimum wage €1,050 -> total employer cost €1,223.25 (incl. €173.25 health insurance).

Mandatory benefits

  • Sick leave first 42 days: paid by employer (at least 70% of average net wage); after 42 days paid by HZZO health fund.
  • Year-end / holiday bonuses: tax-free cap €700 / year (Christmas / Easter / vacation, etc.).
  • One-time retirement compensation: tax-free cap €1,500.
  • Transport allowance / meal allowance: common benefits with tax-free limits.
  • Voluntary pension: employer contributions to Croatian pension funds are tax-free up to €67 / month.

Personal Income Tax

Two progressive brackets + municipal surtax; employer withholds monthly (JOPPD filing).

Personal allowance: €600 / month (€7,200 / year); spouse +€500, each child +€300 – 500.

  • Zagreb municipal surtax is 18% (highest nationwide) → effective top tax rate ~35.4%.
  • Under age 25: 100% exemption on the lower tax bracket; age 26–30: 50% exemption on the lower tax bracket (youth employment incentive).
  • Returning diaspora: 100% income tax exemption for 5 years (must have lived abroad for more than 2 years).

Overtime pay

  • Overtime pay rate is determined by collective agreement or employment contract (no unified legal multiplier).
  • Sunday work: at least +50% wage (statutory minimum).
  • Night work (22:00 – 06:00) and holiday work: additional compensation based on collective agreement.

Contract must be signed in writing (Croatian language); must be concluded before work starts; during probation, 7 days notice is sufficient for termination.

Dismissal protection and severance

  • Dismissal must have a valid reason (business / personal / conduct reason); written notice must state the reason.
  • Notice period: 2 weeks to 3 months (increases with service length); age 50+ / 20+ years of service adds +2 weeks to +1 month.
  • Severance: each completed year = 1/3 month average gross wage; capped at 6 months average gross wage (separate tax-free cap applies).

Key Regulations

Labour Act (Zakon o radu)

2023 amended version; regulates contracts, working hours, leave, dismissal protection, remote work, the right to disconnect, and other core employment relationships.

Eurozone + Schengen (from January 2023)

Croatia has joined the Eurozone and Schengen Area; salaries / social security are denominated in EUR; EU labour mobility applies.

Youth employment tax incentives

Under age 25: 100% exemption on the lower tax bracket; age 26–30: 50% exemption; encourages young people to remain employed in Croatia.

Returning diaspora tax exemption

Croatian citizens who have lived abroad for more than 2 years and return to work in Croatia receive 100% income tax exemption for 5 years.

Disability employment obligation

Employers with 20+ employees must employ at least 3% persons with disabilities; if not met, penalty is 20% of minimum wage per person per month (€210).
