Scope & Application

Applicable Scope and Legal Basis / Polish Labour Code · ZUS Act · Personal Income Tax Act

This document applies to:

Companies that have established a local legal entity in Poland (Sp. z o.o. / S.A. / Sp. j. / Sp. k.), registered with the KRS (Krajowy Rejestr Sądowy, National Court Register)
Employees directly employed by the local entity under an employment contract (umowa o pracę) in accordance with the Labour Code (Kodeks pracy)
Payroll compliance based on the Labour Code, the Social Insurance Act (Ustawa o systemie ubezpieczeń społecznych), and the Personal Income Tax Act (Ustawa o PIT)

MARCO INSIGHT

Poland’s Payroll operates across three independent parallel systems: ZUS (social security) + NFZ (health insurance) + Urząd Skarbowy (tax office). The same salary requires separate filing and payment to 3 different institutions, with staggered deadlines (ZUS DRA by the 15th of the following month, PIT by the 20th of the following month). The combined contribution base for pension + disability is capped annually at PLN 282,600. Once the cap is reached, the corresponding contributions cease for the remaining months of the year, but work injury, sick leave, and health insurance contributions continue.

MARCO INSIGHT

Three commonly underestimated details about Poland’s ZUS / health insurance: (1) The combined annual contribution base cap for pension + disability is PLN 282,600 (approximately PLN 23,550/month). Once the cap is reached, the corresponding contributions cease, but work injury, sick leave, and health insurance continue. (2) The 9% health insurance has not been deductible from PIT since the 2022 Polski Ład reform. (3) Employees under 26 are exempt from PIT, but ZUS and health insurance contributions still apply.

MARCO INSIGHT

Three key changes for Poland Payroll in 2026: (1) Minimum wage increased to PLN 4,806 on 1 January 2026 (+3%, hourly PLN 31.40), with a single annual adjustment rather than the mid-year double adjustments seen in 2023-2024. (2) ZUS annual contribution base cap increased to PLN 282,600 (from PLN 260,190). (3) Christmas Eve (24 December) became a new statutory public holiday from 2025 onwards, increasing the total annual public holidays from 13 to 14 days. It is recommended to complete a full Payroll system update before the end of June.
