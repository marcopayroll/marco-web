Salary Benchmark

Statutory Minimum Wage (Effective January 1, 2026)

  • Minimum hourly wage: KRW 10,320 (+2.9%), monthly approx. KRW 2,156,880 (209h / month)
  • First tripartite unanimous approval since 2008 (No dissenting votes)
  • National unified rate (No regional differences), approx. USD 1,570 / month

Employer Total Additional Cost (Including Four Major Insurance + Severance)

Approximately 18 – 20% of employee annual salary (Including four major insurance ~10% + Severance reserve 8.33% = 1/12 annual salary)

Mandatory Benefits / Common Benefits

  • Severance Pay (퇴직금): Completing 1 year of service = 30 days average wages (Statutory mandatory)
  • Retirement Pension (DC / DB): May replace severance lump-sum payment; employer contribution ≥ 1/12 annual salary
  • Year-End Bonus (성과급 / 상여금): Common but not statutory; usually 100 – 400% monthly salary
  • Meal Allowance (식대): Monthly KRW 200,000 tax-exempt cap (Raised from 2023)

Overtime (연장근로수당)

  • Extended work (overtime): +50% regular wages
  • Night work (22:00 – 06:00): +50% (Stackable with OT = +100%)
  • Rest day work: +50% (8h or under) / +100% (Over 8h)
  • Workplaces under 5 employees not subject to overtime premium rules (Labor Standards Act partially excluded)

Contracts must be signed in written form (Korean); must specify wages / working hours / leave / workplace and other core conditions

Dismissal Protection and Compensation

  • Dismissal must have "justifiable reason" (정당한 사유); 30 days advance notice or pay 30 days average wages (해고예고수당)
  • Severance Pay: Completing 1 year = 30 days average wages (Including all remuneration), available for both resignation and dismissal
  • Unfair dismissal: May apply to Labor Relations Commission for remedy (Reinstatement + back pay)
  • From October 2025 intentional wage arrears may result in 3× punitive damages

Key Regulations

National Pension Reform (Effective January 2026)

Contribution rate increases from 9% by +0.5% annually to 13% by 2033; birth credits and military service credits increasing; extending fund sustainability

52-Hour Workweek Cap

Weekly maximum 52 hours (Statutory 40h + OT 12h); violations subject to criminal liability; flexible working hours system available in 3 – 6 month units

Severance System (퇴직금)

Completing 1 year = 30 days average wages (Mandatory); convertible to retirement pension (DC / DB); lump-sum or annuity payment upon separation

Foreigner 19% Flat Tax

Foreign nationals may choose 19% flat rate (No deductions); applicable first 5 years (Eligible conditions extendable to 20 years)

Non-Regular Worker Protection Law

Contract workers exceeding 2 years treated as regular employees; dispatch maximum 2 years; from 2025 intentional wage arrears subject to 3× punitive damages
