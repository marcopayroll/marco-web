Salary Benchmark

Provincial Minimum Wage UMP (Effective January 2026)

  • DKI Jakarta: Rp 5,729,876 / month (+6.17%), national highest
  • National 36 provinces UMP range: Rp 2.3M (West Java lowest) to Rp 5.73M (Jakarta highest)
  • Formula: Inflation + (Economic growth × Alpha); Alpha range 0.5 – 0.9 (PP 49/2025)

Employer Total Additional Cost (Including BPJS + THR + Severance Provisions)

Approximately 15 – 25% of employee monthly salary (Including BPJS ~11% + THR 1 month 8.33% + Severance provisions)

Mandatory Benefits

  • THR Religious Holiday Allowance: 1 month salary (Completing 12 months); paid 7 days before Hari Raya; under 1 year prorated
  • Severance Pay (Pesangon): Maximum 9 months salary (By service years multiplied) + Long service pay + Compensation
  • Annual Leave Cash-out: Unused annual leave must be compensated in cash
  • BPJS universal mandatory enrollment: Health + Employment insurance; employer must register within 30 days of joining

Personal Income Tax (PPh 21)

  • Progressive 5 tiers (5% – 35%); employer monthly withholding
  • PTKP Exemption: Single Rp 54M / year; married +Rp 4.5M; per child +Rp 4.5M (Maximum 3 children)

Dismissal Protection and Severance Pay

  • Severance Pay (Pesangon): Maximum 9 months salary × multiplier (0.5× – 1× by dismissal reason)
  • Long Service Pay (UPMK): From 3 years of service, maximum 10 months salary
  • Compensation (UPH): Unused annual leave + Relocation costs + Medical expenses etc.

Key Regulations

Cipta Kerja Omnibus Law (UU 6/2023)

Replacing some provisions of the 2003 Labor Law; expanding PKWT usage, adjusting severance pay multipliers, simplifying permit process

PP 49/2025 Wage Regulation

Revised minimum wage calculation formula; Alpha range 0.5 – 0.9; governors must announce UMP before December 24

THR Religious Holiday Allowance

Mandatory payment of 1 month salary; violation fined maximum Rp 500M or 5% annual interest; applicable to all religious holidays

Foreign Worker Work Permit (RPTKA / IMTA)

Must obtain Ministry of Manpower approval; must hire Indonesian assistants and provide training and technology transfer
