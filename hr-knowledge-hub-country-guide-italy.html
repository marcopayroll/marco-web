Salary Benchmark

Minimum Wage - Set by Industry Collective Agreement (CCNL)

  • Italy is one of the few EU countries without a statutory minimum wage (along with Denmark, Austria, Finland, Sweden)
  • Wages are set by industry collective agreements (CCNL / Contratto Collettivo Nazionale di Lavoro)
  • CCNL minimum hourly reference: Cleaning €6.52 / Tourism €7.48 / Commerce €8.50 / Metalworking €9+
  • Union long-term proposal: Statutory minimum €9 / hour (Not yet legislated); EU directive requires coverage guarantee by 2026

Mandatory Benefits

  • TFR Severance Pay (Trattamento di Fine Rapporto): Annual accrual of 6.9% of RAL, lump-sum payment upon separation
  • 13th Salary (Tredicesima): Paid in December, mandatory for all employees; equivalent to additional 1 month salary
  • 14th Salary (Quattordicesima): Paid in June, required by most CCNL (Commerce / Tourism etc.)
  • Public Healthcare (SSN - Servizio Sanitario Nazionale): Universal coverage, employer not required to pay additional health insurance
  • Supplementary Pension (Previdenza Complementare): Optional, TFR can be redirected to supplementary pension fund
  • Meal Vouchers (Buoni Pasto): Common benefit, €8/day (Paper) or €4/day (Electronic) tax-exempt

Personal Income Tax (IRPEF)

  • Italian tax residents must pay IRPEF on worldwide income; employer monthly withholding
  • 2026 reform: Middle tier reduced from 35% to 33% (FY 2026 Finance Law, Law No.199/2025)

All contracts must be in written form per Italian law; specifying CCNL level, salary, working hours, probation period, notice period etc.

Dismissal Protection and Termination

  • Indefinite term contract dismissal requires just cause (Giusta Causa) or justified reason (Giustificato Motivo)
  • Unjustified dismissal: Employees hired after 2015 subject to “Tutele Crescenti” (Increasing Protections), compensation 2-36 months salary
  • Notice period: By CCNL and tenure usually 1 - 4 months; Dirigente can be 6 - 12 months; TFR severance pay must be paid in all forms of termination

Key Regulations

CCNL Collective Agreement System

Approximately 900+ CCNL covering 80%+ of workers; employers must select the applicable industry CCNL, which determines wages / overtime / leave

Jobs Act Reform (2015)

Indefinite term contracts subject to “Tutele Crescenti” (Increasing Protections) system; simplified dismissal procedure, compensation increases by tenure (2-36 months)

Regime Forfettario (Small-Scale Tax Regime)

Self-employed with annual income < €85,000 eligible for flat 15% tax rate (New businesses first 5 years 5%)

Smart Working

From 2022 requires written agreement (Accordo Individuale); must specify work location / right to disconnect / safety obligations

EU Minimum Wage Directive

EU 2022/2041 requires member states to guarantee adequate pay coverage by 2024; Italy aims to meet the target through CCNL coverage rate, but debate continues
