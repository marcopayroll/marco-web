Salary Benchmark

Azerbaijan’s economy is led by oil and gas · non-oil sectors (IT / finance) are growing rapidly

  • Minimum wage AZN 400 / month (from 2024/1 +12.5%) · no adjustment announced for 2026 (actively debated).
  • National average monthly salary AZN 1,022 (gross · 2025 SSC) · median AZN 820 · Baku leads by 30%.
  • 13th salary / Bonus: not statutory · common in banking / oil / multinational / state-owned companies at 1–2 months.

Mandatory / Common Benefits

  • Non-oil PIT incentive (2019–2026): monthly wages ≤ AZN 8,000 in the private non-oil sector are taxed at 0% PIT · strongly attracts foreign investment
  • Supplemental medical insurance: not mandatory · commonly provided by banks / oil companies / multinationals (Pasha / Atesgah)
  • Year-end bonus (13th salary): not statutory · common in banking / oil / IT / multinationals
  • Transport / meal / training allowances: set by collective agreements · partly tax-exempt

Overtime – Labour Code Art. 165

  • Weekday overtime = hourly wage × 2.0 (base 1.0 + overtime 1.0)
  • Rest day / public holiday overtime = hourly wage × 2.0 + 1 day compensatory leave (or additional 1 day wage)
  • Monthly overtime cap 60 hours + annual cap 240 hours · employee written consent required
  • Senior / package-pay roles (Untimed): usually exempt from overtime by contract

Dismissal Protection and Compensation

  • Dismissal must have statutory grounds: business restructuring / incompetence / misconduct / contract expiry
  • Notice period: business restructuring 2 months · incompetence 1 month · misconduct immediate
  • Severance: business restructuring = average monthly salary × service coefficient (1–3 months salary)
  • Pregnant women / single mothers / union representatives: unilateral dismissal is prohibited (except company liquidation)

Key Regulations

Labour Code (1999 + 2024 amendments) — main labour law

Core labour law of Azerbaijan · covers working hours / wages / dismissal / leave · enforced by SLI State Labour Inspectorate.

Non-oil Sector PIT Incentive (2019–2026) — 7-year tax exemption

Private non-oil monthly wages ≤ AZN 8,000 enjoy 0% PIT · strongly attracts foreign investment · proposed extension to 2030.

EISS Electronic Employment Contracts — mandatory registration system

Since 2017, all employment contracts must be electronically registered in EISS within 5 days · unregistered employment is illegal · fines up to AZN 5,000.

Pension Reform 2017 — retirement age increase

Men 65 / women 60 (2026 phase-in · until 2027) · cumulative pension system (NPF).

Personal Data Protection (2010) — data protection

Basic GDPR elements · employer must inform employees of data collection · cross-border transfer requires approval · regulated by State Service.

ASAN Service / Digital Government — digital government

One-stop e-government · employment / tax / social security digitalization · reduces administrative burden.
