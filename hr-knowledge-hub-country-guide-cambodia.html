Salary Benchmark

Cambodia dual currency economy (KHR + USD), majority of employees paid in USD. Garment / Tourism / Construction as pillars.

  • Minimum wage USD 210 / Month (KHR 861,000, 2026/1), only applicable to GFT (Garment / Footwear / Travel goods)
  • Other industries have no unified minimum, usually set by region / industry discretion, general workers USD 180-250
  • 13 Salary / Quarterly bonus / Pchum Ben allowance: Partially company-decided, not statutory but common practice

Employer Additional Cost Summary (Domestic Employee, as % of Base Salary)

NSSF Risk 0.8%, Healthcare 2.6%, Pension 2%, FBT (On demand) 20%

Employer total additional cost is approximately 5-7% of base salary, relatively low in Asia. Plus Seniority Indemnity (Quarterly payment) totaling approximately 11%

Mandatory Benefits / Common Benefits

  • Seniority Indemnity (Seniority Allowance): Quarterly payment of 7.5 days salary (per year 30 days), statutory, since 2019
  • Medical supplement: Not mandatory, medium and large enterprises / Multinational (Forte / Infinity) common
  • 13 Salary / Pchum Ben allowance: Not statutory, some companies decide. Garment industry mandatory, banking / multinational common
  • Transport / Meal / Attendance bonus: Approximately company-decided, garment industry USD 30-50 / month

Overtime (Overtime), Labour Law Art. 139

  • Workday overtime = Hourly rate × 1.50 (First 2 hours and beyond)
  • Night overtime (22:00 - 05:00) = Hourly rate × 2.0
  • Rest day / Public holiday overtime = Hourly rate × 2.0
  • Senior / Management positions: Usually exempt from overtime by contract agreement

Termination Protection and Compensation

  • Termination must have statutory grounds (Labour Law Art. 74-83): Serious misconduct / Business adjustment / Incompetence, requires MoLVT approval (Collective layoff)
  • Notice period: Monthly salary < 6 months 7 days, 6 months-2 years 15 days, 2-5 years 1 month, ≥5 years 2 months, ≥10 years 3 months
  • Severance allowance UDC: Seniority Indemnity already paid quarterly, no additional amount upon separation
  • FDC Severance allowance: 5% of total salary during service period, economic damages (Damages)

Key Regulations

Labour Law 1997 (2018 Amendment) — Labour Law Main Body

Cambodia labor law core, covering working hours / wages / termination / leave. Enforced by MoLVT and Labour Arbitration Council.

NSSF Pension Scheme (2022/10) — Cumulative Pension

2022/10 newly introduced, three-phase 2% → 3.5% → 5.25% (2032), mandatory for all members (Including garment industry).

Seniority Indemnity (2019) — Seniority Allowance

Replacing former UDC severance allowance, quarterly payment of 7.5 days salary / quarter. Settled in full upon employee separation.

Minimum Wage Annual Review — Minimum Wage Annual Assessment

LAC + Unions + Employer tripartite negotiation. Only GFT industry has mandatory minimum wage, other industries set by industry discretion.

Foreign Worker Quota (10%) — Foreign Worker Quota

Domestic employees ≥ 90%, foreign ≤ 10% (Senior technical / Management positions exempt from application).

eFiling / GDT E-Tax System — Digital Taxation

GDT mandatory electronic filing. TOS / VAT / FBT monthly tax reporting. Simplified compliance. Penalties digitally enforced.
