Salary Benchmark

Statutory Minimum Wage (Effective January 2026, Hourly System)

  • 21+ Minimum hourly wage: €14.71 / Hour (+2.16%), adjusted semi-annually (January and July)
  • Including 8% Holiday Allowance (Vakantiegeld) actual hourly wage: €15.89, EU 3rd highest
  • 2024 onwards abolished monthly wage system, unified to hourly system; under 21 reduced by age

Employer Total Additional Cost (Including Social Insurance + 8% Holiday Allowance + Pension)

Approximately 30 - 40% of employee gross wages (Including social insurance ~20% + Holiday Allowance 8% + Pension ~10-15%)

Mandatory Benefits

  • Holiday Allowance (Vakantiegeld): 8% of gross wages, usually paid as lump sum annually in May
  • Sick Leave first 2 years: Employer pays at least 70% wages (Usually 100% in first year); after 2 years transfers to WIA disability insurance
  • Pension: Most industries managed by industry pension funds (ABP / PFZW etc.); employer usually bears 2/3
  • 30% Ruling: Highly skilled foreign employees can enjoy 30% wages tax-free (Maximum 5 years; cap €262,000)

Overtime

  • Netherlands law does not set unified overtime premium rate; determined by CAO or employment contract
  • Common CAO standards: Workday overtime +25-50%; Weekend +50-100%; Holidays +100-200%
  • Night shift allowance: Per CAO determined, usually +25-50%

Dismissal Protection and Compensation

  • Dismissal must go through UWV approval (Economic reasons) or court permission (Personal reasons); must have reasonable grounds
  • Transition Compensation (Transitievergoeding): Per year of service = 1/3 month gross wages; no cap on years but capped amount
  • 2026 cap: €94,000 or 1 year gross wages (Whichever higher)

Key Regulations

Civil Code Book 7 (Burgerlijk Wetboek Boek 7)

Netherlands labor law core; regulating contracts, working hours, leave, dismissal protection, sick leave obligations etc.

VBAR Act (Effective July 2026)

Clarifying employment relationship determination criteria; hourly wage < €36 presumed to be employment relationship; cracking down on bogus self-employment (schijnzelfstandigheid)

30% Ruling (Expatriate Tax Ruling)

Highly skilled foreign employees 30% wages tax-free (Maximum 5 years); 2026 salary cap €262,000; transition period already eliminated

EU Pay Transparency Directive (Effective June 2026)

Enterprises must disclose salary data; narrow gender pay gap; 250+ employee enterprises must submit regular reports

WKR Tax-Free Allowance Space

Employer can provide tax-free benefits (2026 allowance approximately 1.5-2% of total wages); excess portion taxed at 80% penalty rate
