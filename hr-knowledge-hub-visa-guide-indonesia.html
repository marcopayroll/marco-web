Types of Work Visas

Indonesia work-related visas are jointly managed by the Ministry of Manpower (Kemnaker) and the Directorate General of Immigration (Ditjen Imigrasi). Kemnaker handles the Foreign Worker Utilization Plan (RPTKA) and work notification (Notifikasi / IMTA), while Ditjen Imigrasi handles the visa (VITAS) and stay permit (KITAS / e-ITAS). The full process has been digitalized through TKA Online, OSS RBA, and evisa.imigrasi.go.id. The main 2026 work-related stay permits are:

Visa TypeWho It Applies ToDescription (2026)
E23 Work KITASForeign professionals employed by an employer in IndonesiaMainstream route; full RPTKA + Notifikasi + DKP-TKA process required; up to 2 years (issued according to 6 / 12 / 24-month contract cycle), renewable; requires 5+ years relevant work experience.
E28A Investor KITASForeign shareholders holding at least IDR 10 billion (~USD 650k) in a PT PMAExempt from RPTKA and DKP-TKA; issued based on shareholding; up to 2 years, renewable; may perform shareholder functions but not receive employee salary.
E28C / Golden Visa (5 years)High-net-worth individual investors / overseas talentIndividual investment in government bonds / bank deposits / listed company shares of USD 350k; 5-year term; family members may accompany.
E28D / Golden Visa (10 years)High-net-worth individual investors / overseas talentIndividual investment of USD 700k; 10-year term; companies investing USD 25 million may sponsor directors / executives for a 5-year term.
E33G Remote Work KITASPeople employed by overseas companies and working remotely from IndonesiaAnnual income >= USD 60,000; bank balance >= USD 2,000; employer must be registered outside Indonesia; may not serve Indonesian clients; 1-year term, renewable once.
E31 Dependent KITASSpouse / children under 18 of the main applicantValidity follows the main permit; cannot work independently and must obtain a separate work KITAS to be employed.

E23 Work KITAS is the primary pathway for foreign employees hired by Indonesian companies. This guide focuses on the full E23 process: RPTKA + Notifikasi + KITAS. E28A Investor KITAS and E33G Remote Work KITAS have major differences in documents and procedures and should be handled under separate specialized guidance.

Applicant Requirements

1. Employee-side Requirements

(1) E23 Work KITAS

At least 5 years of cumulative work experience related to the proposed position (mandatory from 2026).
Education and professional certificates must match the position description in the RPTKA; Kemnaker will cross-check through international databases.
Monthly salary must reach “expert-level” standards. Industry practice is usually not lower than IDR 15,000,000–30,000,000 (~USD 1,000–2,000), and the salary declared in the RPTKA applies.
Age is usually not below 30; some roles such as directors / executives may be relaxed.
Valid passport with remaining validity >= 18 months, leaving sufficient time for renewal.
No major criminal record and no Indonesian immigration violation history.

(2) E28A Investor KITAS

Direct personal shareholding of at least IDR 10 billion in an Indonesian PT PMA (foreign investment limited liability company).
The company must be registered in OSS RBA and hold a valid NIB (Nomor Induk Berusaha).
May serve as director / commissioner / shareholder, but may not receive salary as an “employee”.

(3) E33G Remote Work KITAS

Annual income proof >= USD 60,000 (past 3 months payslips or tax filing).
Bank account balance >= USD 2,000 (latest 3 months statements).
Valid employment contract with an employer registered outside Indonesia.
Work must not serve Indonesian clients or Indonesian companies.

2. Employer-side Requirements

The company must be a legally registered Indonesian entity, hold a valid NIB (Nomor Induk Berusaha), and be activated in OSS RBA.
The KBLI business code for the proposed role must match the company’s business scope.
Pass Kemnaker’s HPK RPTKA feasibility assessment and justify why the role requires a foreign worker instead of local talent.
Appoint at least 1 Indonesian counterpart employee (Tenaga Kerja Pendamping) to receive technology / knowledge transfer training.
Pay DKP-TKA foreign worker compensation fund monthly: USD 100 / person / month, prepaid in one lump sum according to the approved RPTKA period.
Directors / commissioners (Direksi / Komisaris) must also be filed through RPTKA when employed.

Important Reminder

From 2026, Kemnaker has strengthened verification of “education + 5 years of experience”. The applicant’s degree / professional certificates must closely match the RPTKA job description and will be cross-checked against international education databases. Inflated resumes or mismatched education may lead to direct RPTKA rejection, and the same position cannot be resubmitted for 6 months.

Application Document Checklist

1. Employee Documents

Valid passport (remaining validity >= 18 months) + scanned biodata page.
Recent color ID photo (white background, 4×6 cm, JPG electronic version).
Education certificate (bachelor’s degree or above) + Chinese Ministry of Education authentication or international authentication; Apostille required (China applicable from 2023.11).
Professional qualification / industry license certificate (if applicable).
Resume with at least 5 years of role-related work experience + former employer reference letter / employment certificate.
Police clearance certificate (issued by Chinese public security authority, Apostille).
Health examination report (including HIV, hepatitis, tuberculosis screening; some consulates require pre-entry submission).
Accompanying family: marriage certificate, children’s birth certificates (Apostille + Indonesian / English translation).
BPJS Kesehatan registration proof (to be completed within 6 months after entry).

2. Employer Documents

Company NIB (Nomor Induk Berusaha) + OSS RBA registration proof.
Company deed of establishment (Akta Pendirian) + approval letter from the Ministry of Law and Human Rights.
Latest annual financial statements + tax registration certificate (NPWP).
HPK RPTKA feasibility assessment materials (job description, reason for hiring foreign worker, local talent training plan).
Pengesahan RPTKA (Kemnaker approval).
DKP-TKA payment proof: USD 100 / month × approved RPTKA months.
Employment contract + KTP copy of the Indonesian counterpart employee (Tenaga Pendamping).
Employment contract for the proposed foreign employee (position, salary, start date, contract term).
Company sponsor letter (Surat Sponsor) + legal representative signature and company stamp.

Note: Apostille and Indonesian Translation

Indonesia is strict about education authentication. Since China officially joined the Hague Convention in November 2023, Chinese education / notarized documents can be Apostilled directly without dual legalization by the Indonesian embassy. However, all documents must still be translated into Indonesian or English and signed / stamped by a legally recognized Indonesian sworn translator (Penerjemah Tersumpah). It is recommended to complete Apostille + translation in China before sending documents.

Detailed Steps

1.The employer first confirms in OSS RBA that the NIB is valid and the KBLI code for the role matches the company business scope. If needed, the business scope can be amended in OSS at the same time.
2.The employer logs in to TKA Online (tka-online.kemnaker.go.id) to submit HPK RPTKA: upload job description, reason for hiring a foreign worker, local training plan, and Indonesian counterpart employee information. Kemnaker usually provides feasibility assessment feedback within 7–14 days.
3.After HPK approval, the Pengesahan RPTKA second-stage approval begins: upload full RPTKA documents, company materials, foreign employee passport and CV. First-time approval usually takes 2–4 weeks; renewals are faster.
4.After RPTKA approval, prepay DKP-TKA by approved months: USD 100 × months (for 12 months, USD 1,200). Payment proof is automatically synchronized to TKA Online and triggers issuance of the individual work notification (Notifikasi / electronic IMTA).
5.The employee or employer submits the work VITAS e-visa application at evisa.imigrasi.go.id. Data is automatically pulled from TKA Online; e-VITAS (PDF) is usually issued in 5–10 working days and is valid for entry within 90 days.
6.The employee enters Indonesia with e-VITAS. At the airport immigration counter, QR code confirmation is sufficient; no visa sticker is required. Keep both electronic and printed copies.
7.Within 30 days after entry, the employee must visit the immigration office (Kantor Imigrasi) at the work location to activate e-ITAS and complete biometrics (fingerprints, face, signature). The e-ITAS is issued electronically through the APAPO system.
8.Also within 30 days, report to the local police station for STM (Surat Tanda Melapor temporary residence report). Some cities require SKTT (foreign resident certificate) before STM.
9.For multiple exits during KITAS validity, apply for MERP (Multiple Exit Re-Entry Permit) together with e-ITAS to avoid applying for ERP each time.

Note: Fully Online Process

The full process is already online. TKA Online (Kemnaker), OSS RBA (investment authority), and evisa.imigrasi.go.id (Directorate General of Immigration) are interconnected. E-VITAS, e-ITAS, and Notifikasi are all issued electronically; embassy visa stickers are no longer required. However, arrival activation (biometrics) and police STM reporting still require in-person attendance. For compliance, it is recommended to engage a qualified local Indonesian immigration agent (Agen Visa) to assist with the process.

Arrival Guide

Important Reminder: 30-Day Deadlines After Entry

After entering with e-VITAS, the employee must complete KITAS (e-ITAS) activation and biometrics within 30 days, and file the STM police report within 30 days. Missing either deadline may result in a fine of IDR 1,000,000 per day (~USD 65). Continuous non-compliance may lead to immigration blacklisting and deportation.

1. Work-Related

Within 30 days after entry: go to the immigration office at the work location to activate KITAS, provide biometrics, and receive the e-ITAS electronic stay permit.
Within 30 days after entry: obtain STM (temporary residence report) at the local police station. Some cities require SKTT (foreign resident certificate) from the district office (Kelurahan) first.
Tax registration: apply for personal NPWP (taxpayer number) with the Directorate General of Taxes (DJP) within 30 days after entry. Monthly salary above IDR 54 million is subject to the highest 35% PPh 21 withholding bracket.
Social security (mandatory): for KITAS holders staying >= 6 months, the employer must enroll them in BPJS Ketenagakerjaan (JKK work injury 0.24–1.74%, JKM death 0.30%, JHT pension savings 5.7%). Foreign employees are exempt from JP pension insurance.
Health insurance (mandatory): employer must enroll the employee in BPJS Kesehatan; employer 4%, employee 1%, capped at monthly salary IDR 12 million.
PPh 21 personal income tax is withheld monthly under the TER mechanism (from 2024): monthly income IDR 0–54 million uses a 5–30% progressive range; annual income above IDR 5 billion applies the top 35% bracket.
For stays exceeding 5 years and meeting conditions, apply for KITAP permanent stay permit (requires 3 consecutive renewals of work KITAS, totaling 5 years).

2. Daily Life

Open a local bank account: BCA, Mandiri, BNI, BRI, CIMB Niaga, etc.; requires KITAS + passport + NPWP + employer introduction letter.
Housing: use Rumah123, Rumah.com, 99.co, OLX, etc.; Jakarta / Bali have strong supply, monthly rent often includes furniture, deposit usually 1–3 months.
Transport: Jakarta MRT / LRT / TransJakarta network is developed; JakLingko card is widely used. Other cities mainly rely on taxi / Gojek / Grab.
SIM card: Telkomsel, Indosat, XL Axiata, Smartfren; real-name registration with KITAS + passport is required, otherwise the number may be deactivated within 30 days.
Mobile payment: GoPay, OVO, DANA, ShopeePay, QRIS QR code payments are nationwide; credit card acceptance is moderate, so keep both options.
Driver licence: Chinese licence + translated notarization may be used temporarily for 3 months; after that, apply for Indonesian SIM A (car) or SIM C (motorcycle).
Cultural note: Indonesia is Muslim-majority; during Ramadan, be mindful with public eating and alcohol. Bali and Jakarta are relatively more open.

3. Dependent Visas

Spouse (legally registered marriage) and unmarried children under 18 may apply for E31 Dependent KITAS, issued and expiring together with the main applicant’s KITAS.
E31 Dependent KITAS holders cannot legally work in Indonesia. To work, they must obtain an independent work KITAS (E23).
Dependents must also complete e-ITAS activation + STM police report within 30 days after entry.
Children studying in Indonesia may choose international schools (Jakarta JIS, SPH, BIS; Bali Green School, etc.), with annual tuition typically USD 15k–35k.
Dependents can be covered under BPJS Kesehatan; employer contribution based on the main employee’s salary 4% covers up to 5 immediate family members: spouse + 3 children.
Spouse / children / parents of Golden Visa (E28C / E28D) holders may apply for dependent stay permits together; this is one of the few work-related visa types allowing parents to accompany.

Existing KITAS: Changing Employers

Indonesia KITAS is strictly linked to the RPTKA and specific employer. When an employee changes employer, there is no simplified “change employer” route. The original employer must first process EPO (Exit Permit Only) to cancel the old KITAS, the employee must leave Indonesia, and the new employer must restart the full RPTKA + Notifikasi + VITAS + KITAS process. This is one of the biggest differences between Indonesia’s work visa system and systems in Germany, the Netherlands, and other EU countries.

1. Core Rules

KITAS TypeEmployer Change Rule
E23 Work KITASStrongly bound to employer and position; changing employer requires EPO cancellation of old KITAS, departure, and new employer restarting the full process.
E28A Investor KITASBound to shareholding company; if shareholding structure / company changes, reapplication is required; if shareholder status remains unchanged, renewal is possible.
E33G Remote Work KITASBound to overseas employer; changing overseas employer requires reapplication with new contract and income proof; does not affect local residence in the same way.
E28C / E28D Golden VisaNot tied to an employer; holder may freely work / start a business if investment threshold is maintained.
E31 Dependent KITASFollows the main applicant; when the main applicant’s KITAS is cancelled, the dependent KITAS becomes invalid at the same time.
KITAP Permanent Stay PermitFully free, with no employer restriction; holder may work / start a business / invest freely.

2. Practical Steps to Change Employers

1.Employee signs employment contract with the new employer and confirms the new employer meets RPTKA application requirements (NIB / KBLI / financial compliance).
2.Original employer submits EPO (Exit Permit Only) application to immigration: the original KITAS is officially cancelled, and the employee must leave Indonesia within 7 days after EPO issuance.
3.Original employer also reports the employee departure in TKA Online and cancels the original RPTKA / Notifikasi. Prepaid DKP-TKA is usually non-refundable.
4.After the employee leaves Indonesia, the new employer submits a new HPK RPTKA + Pengesahan RPTKA + DKP-TKA payment through TKA Online.
5.After the new Notifikasi is issued, the employee applies for a new work VITAS through evisa.imigrasi.go.id; once approved, the employee re-enters with the new VITAS.
6.Within 30 days after re-entry, activate the new KITAS and complete biometrics at the immigration office at the new employer’s location; file a new STM at the police station for the new residence address.

Important Reminder: No Grace Period

Indonesia KITAS has no “unemployment grace period”. After employment with the original employer ends, EPO must be processed immediately and the employee must leave within 7 days. Staying in Indonesia while “between jobs” is illegal residence and carries a daily fine of IDR 1,000,000. Between EPO issuance and activation of the new KITAS, there is usually a 4–8 week “legal gap” during which the employee cannot work / reside in Indonesia. Plan the handover with the new employer before the old contract ends, including RPTKA filing, departure, re-entry, and activation.

4. Permanent Stay and Employer Change

E23 Work KITAS holders who have continuously resided for 5 years (3 consecutive renewals, with no interruption caused by job change) may apply for KITAP (permanent stay permit). E28A Investor KITAS holders may convert to KITAP after 3 years; Golden Visa (E28C / E28D) holders may also apply for KITAP after 3 years. KITAP holders are no longer tied to an employer and may work, self-employ, start a business, or invest freely. This is the long-term solution to avoid the “change employer requires full reapplication” problem.

Departure Guide

1.Submit resignation notice according to the employment contract (usually 1 month notice, subject to contract); employer settles final salary + THR (religious holiday allowance, prorated by monthly salary) + unused annual leave cash-out.
2.If the employer terminates the employee and Cipta Kerja Law applies, settle severance compensation (Uang Pesangon) + service award (Uang Penghargaan Masa Kerja) based on seniority tiers.
3.Employer reports the departure in TKA Online and cancels RPTKA and Notifikasi.
4.Submit EPO (Exit Permit Only) application: original passport + old KITAS + company sponsor letter. Immigration usually stamps / issues EPO within 3–5 working days.
5.After EPO issuance, leave Indonesia within 7 days and do not overstay; overstay fine is IDR 1,000,000 per day.
6.BPJS Ketenagakerjaan JHT withdrawal: before departure, apply at BPJS office for lump-sum withdrawal using EPO proof + passport, or keep the account for future work in Indonesia.
7.Tax clearance: submit departure-year personal income tax filing (SPT Tahunan) to DJP and settle tax payable / refund; apply to make NPWP “Non-Efektif” if needed.
8.Close Indonesian bank account: preferably keep it for 1–2 months to settle final transactions; account closure usually requires in-person counter visit with passport + KITAS before cancellation.
9.Cancel subscriptions: SIM card, broadband, lease, vehicle / motorcycle insurance, etc.; actively cancel SIM card or the number may be recycled and affect credit records.
10.Dependents process EPO together: dependent KITAS follows the main permit; dependents must complete departure procedures together with or before the main applicant leaves.
11.If planning to return to Indonesia: NPWP can remain “Non-Efektif”; BPJS account can be retained long term.

Key Policy Changes from 2023 to 2026

Indonesia has recently pushed in two directions: digitalizing and simplifying processes, while strengthening compliance review. The core changes from 2023 to 2026 focus on system integration, long-stay visa creation, and stricter compliance requirements:
