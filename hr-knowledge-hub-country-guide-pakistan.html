Mandatory Benefits / Common Benefits

  • Provident Fund (Provident Fund): 20+ employee enterprises mandatory; employer matches employee basic salary at a set ratio
  • Gratuity (Gratuity): Completing 1 year = 30 days wages per year of service (Paid on separation)
  • Overtime: Regular hourly wage x 2 (Statutory, exceeding 9h / day or 48h / week)
  • Annual Profit Sharing: Some provinces require profit > PKR 500K enterprises to distribute via WWF

Key Regulations

18th Constitutional Amendment (2010)

Labor legislative power devolved to provinces; resulting in different provincial labor laws / minimum wages / social insurance rates; increasing compliance complexity

EOBI Pension System

Nationally unified; employer 5% + employee 1% (Calculated on minimum wage basis, not actual wages); eligible for pension after 15 years

Provincial Social Insurance (PESSI / SESSI)

Varies by province (6-7%); covering medical / work injury / maternity / death; must register with provincial social insurance institutions

Workers’ Welfare Fund (WWF)

Annual profit > PKR 500,000 enterprises must contribute 2% net profit; used for workers’ housing / education / medical

Foreign Worker Work Permit

Must obtain Ministry of Interior (MOI) work visa + Ministry of Labour NOC; IT / Energy industries have simplified procedures
