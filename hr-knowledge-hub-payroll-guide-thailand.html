Scope & Application

This document applies to:

Companies that have established a local legal entity in Thailand (บริษัท จํากัด / Co., Ltd.)
Employees directly employed by the local entity, subject to the Labour Protection Act (พ.ร.บ. คุ้มครองแรงงาน พ.ศ. 2541)
Payroll compliance based on the Revenue Code and the Social Security Act B.E. 2533

This framework explains: how companies complete Payroll Set-up, salary calculation, monthly filing and payment, and annual compliance management in Thailand.

MARCO INSIGHT

Thailand’s Payroll system is relatively straightforward: social security contribution rates are fixed with a low ceiling (THB 15,000 base), and income tax is withheld monthly (ภ.ง.ด.1). The core complexity lies in the severance pay system: Thailand’s statutory severance pay can reach up to 400 days’ wages based on years of service, and is legally mandatory for no-fault termination, making it one of the highest in the Asia-Pacific region.

MARCO INSIGHT

Thailand’s statutory minimum annual leave is only 6 days, but market practice is typically 10-15 days. The 2019 amendment added 3 days of paid personal leave (Business Leave). The cost of 98-day maternity leave is shared roughly equally between the employer and SSO. Payroll must accurately split and track SSO reimbursement.

iv. Provident Fund (PVD) – กองทุนสํารองเลี้ยงชีพ – Voluntary Pension

ItemRule

Tax BenefitsEmployer contributions are tax-deductible; employee contributions enjoy PIT deduction (cap THB 500,000)

MARCO INSIGHT

Thailand’s PIT uses annual progressive rates but with monthly estimated withholding. Employers must estimate total annual taxable income at the beginning of each year (or upon employee onboarding) and spread it evenly across 12 months for withholding. If there is a salary adjustment or bonus change mid-year, the withholding for the remaining months must be recalculated to ensure total annual withholding approximates the actual tax liability.

Termination Operational Key Points:

Notice Period: If not specified in the contract, advance notice of one pay cycle is required (one month for monthly salary employees)
Special Severance Pay: If the company relocates and the employee is unwilling to follow, additional special severance pay must be paid
Termination for Cause (Section 119): Severance pay may be waived in statutory cases of serious misconduct
Settlement Timeline: All outstanding payments must be settled within 3 days of termination
Severance Pay Tax Treatment: Tax-exempt ceiling = last monthly salary × years of service × THB 300,000 rule; amounts exceeding the ceiling are taxable

MARCO INSIGHT

Thailand’s severance pay can reach up to 400 days’ wages (≥20 years of service), making it one of the highest in the Asia-Pacific region. Companies must conduct actuarial valuations of severance pay obligations under TAS 19 / TFRS (Thai Accounting Standards) and recognize provisions in financial statements. The 2019 amendment increased severance pay for ≥20 years of service from 300 days to 400 days, significantly raising long-term employment costs.
