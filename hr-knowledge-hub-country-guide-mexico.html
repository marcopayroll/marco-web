Mandatory Benefits

  • Christmas Bonus (Aguinaldo): >= 15 days wages, must be paid before December 20
  • Holiday Bonus (Prima Vacacional): Annual leave period additional 25% supplement
  • Profit Sharing (PTU): 10% of enterprise taxable profit, distributed to employees before May each year
  • Sunday Work Premium (Prima Dominical): 25% additional wages
  • Meal Vouchers / Food Vouchers (Vales de Despensa): Common benefit, tax-advantaged
  • Savings Fund (Fondo de Ahorro): Employer matches employee savings, cap 13% daily wage

Key Regulations

Outsourcing Reform (2021 Labor Law Amendment)

Prohibiting labor outsourcing (subcontratación de personal); only permitting specialized service outsourcing, must register with REPSE

Electronic Payroll Voucher (CFDI)

Employer must issue electronic payroll receipts through SAT system (CFDI de Nómina), filing on schedule

Profit Sharing PTU Cap

Employee PTU capped at 3 months wages or most recent 3-year PTU average (Whichever higher), 2021 reform newly added

Working Hours Reform Proposal

Planned to gradually reduce from 48 hours to 40 hours / week (2027: 46h → 2028: 44h → 2030: 40h), already submitted to Congress

Electronic Employment Contracts

Electronically signed employment contracts have legal validity; IMSS / INFONAVIT registration must be completed within 5 days of joining
