Common Additional Benefits

  • Christmas Bonus (Weihnachtsgeld): Approx. one month’s salary, by contract / collective agreement
  • Vacation Allowance (Urlaubsgeld): Some industry collective agreements stipulate additional vacation pay
  • Meal Allowance: Approx. €3.10 / day (Tax-exempt within limit)
  • Transport Allowance: Public transit subsidy or €0.30 / km private car commuting allowance
  • Home Office Allowance: Maximum €6 / day, capped at 210 days / year (Maximum €1,260 / year)
  • Supplementary Insurance: Dental, vision, or private supplementary health insurance

Personal Income Tax

  • German tax residents must pay income tax on worldwide income
  • Tax-free Basic Allowance (Grundfreibetrag): €12,348 (2026, Single); married joint filing doubled

Key Regulations

Minimum Wage Record-Keeping Obligation

Employers must record working hours of employees subject to minimum wage, and retain for at least 2 years.

Equal Pay Act

„Entgelttransparenzgesetz“ grants employees the right to inquire about pay for equivalent positions (Applicable to > 200 employee companies).

Digital Reform

„Fourth Bureaucracy Reduction Act“ (2025): Indefinite term contracts may use e-signature; fixed term contracts still require hand signature.

Mini-Job

Monthly income ≤ €556 marginal employment, social insurance fully borne by employer, suitable for part-time workers.

Wage Payment

Paid monthly, must be via bank transfer; must provide written payslip (Gehaltsabrechnung).
