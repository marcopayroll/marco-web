Salary Benchmark

  • In a high-inflation environment, salaries are adjusted quarterly via CCT agreements · dollarization discussions continue · some industries (IT / exports) pay in USD
  • SMVM statutory minimum wage: ARS 357,800 / month (2026/4) · often adjusted every 3–6 months
  • Average monthly wage: ARS 1,400,000 (gross · 2025 INDEC) · median ARS 950,000
  • 13th salary (Sueldo Anual Complementario / SAC): statutory · paid as half-month salary by 6/30 + 12/18

  • Employer total additional cost is ~27 – 32% of basic salary · 13th salary (Aguinaldo) adds +8.33% annualized.

Mandatory / Common Benefits

  • Aguinaldo (13th salary SAC): statutory · paid as half-month salary by 6/30 + 12/18 · cannot be omitted
  • Obra Social: union health plan · assigned by CCT (banking OSDE / commerce OSCE) · covers dependents
  • Vales Alimentarios / Tarjeta: meal voucher / card · common in mid-sized and large companies · non-taxable
  • Year-end bonus (Bono): union agreement · physical gift boxes (Caja Navidad) are common

Overtime Pay (Horas Extras · LCT Art. 200–204)

  • Weekday overtime = hourly wage × 1.50 · overtime applies from the 9th hour
  • Overtime after Saturday 13:00 / Sunday / public holidays = hourly wage × 2.0
  • Annual limit 200 hours · CCT agreements may adjust (limit 30 hours / month)
  • Empleados Jerárquicos senior staff (DGI Category A): usually contractually exempt from overtime

Dismissal Protection and Compensation

  • Dismissal requires legal cause (Causa Justa) or compensation payment (Sin Causa)
  • Notice period: probation 15 days · service < 5 years 1 month · ≥ 5 years 2 months
  • Compensation (Indemnización Art. 245): 1 monthly salary / year of service · capped at 3× agreement wage
  • Illegal dismissal extras: proportional SAC + unused vacation + double compensation (Trabajo No Registrado)

Key Regulations

LCT Ley 20.744 — Main Labour Contract Law

Enacted in 1974 · covers working hours / wages / dismissal / leave · enforced by Ministerio de Trabajo and Justicia del Trabajo · foundation of all individual employment relationships in Argentina

Ley 27.742 (Bases / RIGI) 2024 — Milei Reform

Probation extended to 6–8 months · encourages major investments via RIGI (30-year tax stability) · reduces freelance / employer costs · simplifies some labour obligations for SMEs

CCT (Convenios Colectivos) — Industry Collective Agreements

~600 CCTs cover 80% of employees · wages / benefits negotiated by industry union · inflation-linked adjustments every 3–6 months · sector minimum wages often above SMVM

Ley Bases / Empleo Registrado — Anti-Informal Employment

From 2024, double penalties for illegal (unregistered) employment · targets “Trabajo en Negro” · ARCA (former AFIP) unified inspections from May 2024 · fines can reach 3× monthly wages per worker

Ley 25.326 PDPA — Personal Data Protection

GDPR-equivalent · enforced by AAIP data protection authority · covers employee data collection and processing · fines up to ARS 50 million · data transfer restrictions to non-adequate countries

Ley 24.557 ART — Occupational Risk Insurance

Mandatory commercial work injury insurance (ART) · rate by industry risk level 1–6% · CFR (Comisiones Médicas) commissions handle disputes · employer must register workers before first day of work
