Mandatory benefits / common benefits

  • Meal allowance (Stravenkový paušál): tax-exempt cap CZK 129.50 per shift (cash / meal vouchers both allowed).
  • Recreation benefit tax exemption: health-related CZK 48,967 / year · other leisure benefits CZK 24,484 / year.
  • Sick leave first 14 days: employer pays 60% of average daily wage; after 14 days paid by the social security fund.
  • Supplementary pension: common employer benefit (tax-exempt employer contribution cap CZK 50,000 / year).

Personal Income Tax

Income Tax (Daň z příjmu)

Progressive two brackets: 15% / 23%; employer withholds monthly advance tax.

Basic tax credit: CZK 30,840 / year (taxpayer credit) · spouse CZK 24,840 (must care for a child under 3).

  • Overtime pay: at least +25% of average wage or equivalent time off.

Dismissal protection and severance

  • Dismissal requires a statutory reason (organizational change / health / misconduct, etc.); written notice period is 2 months.
  • Severance pay for organizational dismissal: 1 – 3 years = 1 month; 3+ years = 2 months; maximum 3 months.
  • Employee resignation: notice period is also 2 months; no entitlement to severance pay.

Key Regulations

Labour Code (Zákoník práce · No. 262/2006)

In force since 2006 and amended many times; regulates employment contracts, working hours, leave, dismissal protection, remote work and other core labour relations.

JMHZ unified monthly report (from April 2026)

Employers must submit a unified monthly report to the social security authority; data for January – March must be supplemented by the end of June; it replaces multiple separate filings.

Mandatory pension savings for hazardous work (new 2026 rule)

Employers with risk category 3 roles must contribute 4% of the social security base into employees’ pension savings products; at least 3 shifts per month.

Flexible work: DPP / DPC agreements

DPP monthly income above CZK 12,000 triggers social security contributions (new 2026 threshold); DPC is limited to 20 hours per week.

EU member state + foreign work permits

EU / EEA citizens may work freely; third-country nationals must apply for a work permit + employee card (Zaměstnanecká karta).
