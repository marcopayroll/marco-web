Salary Benchmark

  • NCR minimum wage ₱695 / day (Non-agriculture, 2024/7 onwards, 2026 expected to adjust to ₱750)
  • BPO / IT-BPM industry mainly foreign-invested employers, mid-level engineer monthly salary ₱70,000 – 120,000
  • Minimum wage employees exempt from personal income tax (TRAIN Law), 13th month salary mandatory paid before 12/24 each year

Employer Total Additional Cost (Local Employee, as % of Basic Salary)

SSS 10%, PhilHealth 2.5%, Pag-IBIG 2%, EC 0.1 - 0.3%

Employer total additional cost approximately 13 - 16% of basic salary, additionally including 13th month (8.33%) + leave + medical supplement

Mandatory Benefits / Common Benefits

  • 13th Month Pay (PD 851): Universal mandatory, paid before 12/24 each year, equals full year basic salary / 12
  • Medical Supplement: Medium and large enterprises commonly provide HMO card, covering employees + spouse / dependents
  • 14th Month / Quarterly Bonus: BPO / Banking / Multinational commonly provide, based on performance or contractually agreed
  • Group Life Insurance GPA + Meal Allowance / Communication Allowance / Remote Allowance: 2023 Remote Work Act RA 11165 onwards becoming widespread

Overtime, DOLE LC Art. 87

  • Workday OT = Hourly wage × 125%, applicable when daily exceeds 8 hours / Special holiday OT = Hourly wage × 130%
  • Statutory holiday (Regular) work = Double wages, overtime portion additional 30%
  • Night shift differential = +10% hourly wage (10pm – 6am), stackable with OT
  • Senior management / Field workers / Domestic workers / Salaried (Field Personnel) not subject to OT rules

Dismissal Protection and Compensation

  • Only lawful grounds for dismissal (LC Art. 297-299): Serious breach of contract / Business restructuring / Retrenchment, 30 days written notice required
  • Unfair dismissal compensation: Reinstatement + full back wages (Backwages), no reinstatement pays 1 month / year service fee
  • Severance / Retrenchment pay: 1/2 month salary / year (Business loss) or 1 month salary / year (Equipment replacement / Cost savings)
  • Retirement Pay (RA 7641): Age 60 + 5 years of service, 22.5 days / year (Including 13th month), mandatory

Key Regulations

Labor Code (PD 442) - Labor Law Main Body

1974 promulgated, covering working hours / wages / dismissal / leave / unions, enforced by DOLE and NLRC jointly, 2017 DO 174 strictly regulating labor dispatch

Telecommuting Act (RA 11165) - Remote Work Law

2018 passed, 2023 IRR revised, remote employees entitled to same wages as on-site / benefits / training opportunities, equipment provided by employer

Mental Health Act (RA 11036) - Mental Health Law

2018 mandatory, employer must provide mental health policy / training / referral support, encouraging EAP Employee Assistance Programs

Magna Carta of Women (RA 9710) - Women’s Rights Law

2009 onwards, anti-sexual harassment + equal pay + maternity leave + women’s special surgery leave, companies must establish internal complaints mechanism

Universal Health Care Act (RA 11223) - Universal Health Insurance Law

2019 passed, universal PhilHealth coverage, 2025/1 onwards employer contribution 5% (2.5% equally split), covering inpatient / outpatient

Anti-Endo / DO 174-17 - Anti-Short-Term Cycling Employment

2017 onwards strictly restricting 5-5-5 short-term contracts, dispatch agencies must hold DOLE license, dispatched workers must receive SSS / PH / 13th month
