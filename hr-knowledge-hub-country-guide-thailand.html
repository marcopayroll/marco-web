Salary Benchmark

Statutory minimum wage (by province — daily wage system)

  • Bangkok / Phuket / Chonburi: THB 400 / day (approx. USD 11.5) — the highest national rate
  • Other provinces: THB 337 – 380 / day, with around 17 different provincial rate tiers nationwide
  • Monthly equivalent (Bangkok, 26 working days): approx. THB 10,400 / month or approx. USD 297

Employer statutory social security is only 5% (one of the lowest in Asia) + work injury 0.2–1%. In 2026 the SSF wage cap increases from THB 15,000 to 17,500. Three-stage reform: 2026 cap THB 17,500 → 2029 THB 20,000 → 2032 THB 23,000.

Total additional employer cost (incl. SSF + work injury + provident fund, etc.)

Approx. 7 – 15% of employee gross salary (incl. SSF 5% + work injury ~0.5% + voluntary provident fund 5–10%).

Mandatory / Common Benefits

  • SSF covers: medical care / maternity / disability / unemployment / old-age pension / child allowance / death
  • Sick leave: employer pays the first 30 days in full per year (statutory); medical certificate required for 3+ consecutive days
  • Maternity leave: 98 days — employer pays 45 days + SSF pays 53 days (subject to cap) · 2025 law amendment
  • Paternity leave: 15 days paid leave (new in 2025 · Labour Protection Act Amendment No. 9)

Overtime Pay

  • Weekday overtime: +50% of basic hourly wage (1.5×)
  • Rest-day work during normal hours: +100% (2×); overtime portion +200% (3×)
  • Public holiday work: normal working hours +100%; overtime +200% (plus holiday pay)
  • Late wage payment: 15% annual interest + additional 15% surcharge every 7 days

Key Regulations

Labour Protection Act B.E. 2541

Core Thai labour law; the 2025 amendment (No. 9) introduced 15 days of paternity leave and confirmed 98 days of maternity leave.

2026 Social Security Cap Reform (three stages)

SSF wage cap increases from THB 15,000 → 17,500 (2026) → 20,000 (2029) → 23,000 (2032); the contribution rate remains 5%.

Employee Welfare Fund EWF (expected launch Oct 2026)

New mandatory contribution layer; detailed rules are pending; it will further increase employer cost.

Foreign Worker Work Permit

Requires a Ministry of Labour work permit + Non-Immigrant B visa; for each foreign employee, the employer must hire 4 Thai employees (4:1 ratio).

Severance System (relatively high in Asia)

Employees with 120+ days of service are entitled to severance; 20+ years of service receives up to 400 days of wages; recommended probation period is within 119 days.
