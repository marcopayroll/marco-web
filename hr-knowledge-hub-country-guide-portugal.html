Salary Benchmark

Portugal uses 14-salary system (12 monthly + 6 months vacation allowance + 12 months Christmas allowance), after conversion annual salary is approximately 17% higher than monthly salary

  • RMMG minimum wage EUR 870 (2025), 2026/1 raised to EUR 920 (+5.7%), DL 139/2025
  • From 2026 RMMG minimum wage fully exempt from IRS, maintaining mínimo de existência unchanged
  • Average monthly salary EUR 1,615 (Gross, 2025 INE), median EUR 1,250, Lisboa / Porto leading

Employer Total Additional Cost (Local Employee, as % of Basic Salary)

TSU 23.75%, FCT/FGCT 1.0%, Work Injury ~1.0%, no social insurance cap (High salary fully calculated). Employer total additional cost approximately 26 – 28%, 14-salary system makes annual total cost +17%

Mandatory Benefits / Common Benefits

  • 14-salary system: 12 monthly + 7 months vacation allowance + 12 months Christmas allowance (Statutory, completing 12 months by proportion)
  • Meal Voucher (Subsídio de Alimentação): Mandatory, daily EUR 6.00 (2026 onwards, cash) / EUR 10.20 (Meal card, tax-exempt)
  • Medical Supplement (ADSE / Multicare / Médis): Medium and large enterprises commonly provide, beyond SNS public insurance
  • Travel Allowance / Remote Allowance: From 2022 remote office employees daily EUR 4.77 equipment / internet tax-exempt subsidy

Dismissal Protection and Compensation

  • Redundancy compensation (Despedimento Coletivo): 12 days wages / year (New contracts) or 30 days / year (Pre-2014 contracts)
  • Unlawful dismissal (Despedimento Ilícito): Reinstatement + back pay, if not reinstated must pay 15 – 45 days / year compensation
  • Notice period: Probation 7 days, 1-2 years 30 days, 2-5 years 60 days, > 5 years 75 days
  • Unemployment benefit Subsídio Desemprego: Contributing 360 days qualifying, 65% wages, 5 – 18 months (By service years)

Key Regulations

Código do Trabalho (Lei 7/2009) – Labor Law Main Body

Current labor law core, covering working hours / wages / dismissal / leave / collective agreements, enforced by ACT Labor Department

Decent Work Agenda (Lei 13/2023) – 2023 Decent Work Reform

Abuse of temporary contracts fined EUR 4,800 – 76,800, promoting employment relationship stability, platform worker protection, wage transparency

IFICI / NHR 2.0 (Art. 58-A EBF) – Foreign Research Talent Tax Incentive

From 2024/1 replacing NHR, research / high-tech / teaching foreign nationals enjoy 20% flat rate for 10 years, overseas asset income exempt

Teletrabalho Lei 83/2021 – Remote Work Law

From 2022/1, employees with unilateral right to apply (Children under 8), employer must pay remote allowance (Equipment / Internet daily EUR 4.77 tax-exempt)

Right to Disconnect (CT Art. 199-B)

From 2021/12, employer must not contact employees outside working hours, violators fined, encouraging work-life balance

AIMA – Foreign Work Residence Application New Mechanism

2023/10 SEF dissolved, AIMA takes over, work visa / long-stay / D series visa unified processing, 2024 onwards backlog reduction
