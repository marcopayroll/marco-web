Salary Benchmark

Statutory Minimum Wage (Effective January 2026)

  • Minimum gross wage (Non-qualified positions): HUF 322,800 / month (+11%), approx. EUR 810
  • Guaranteed minimum salary (Technical / Degree positions): HUF 373,200 / month (+7%), approx. EUR 933
  • Target: 2027 minimum wage to reach 50% of national average gross wage

Mandatory Benefits / Common Benefits

  • SZÉP Leisure Card: Employer can deposit up to HUF 570,000 annually; only 28% tax rate (Far lower than cash 46.5%)
  • Small Gifts: Tax-exempt cap HUF 32,280 per occasion (= Minimum wage 10%)
  • Remote Work Allowance: Monthly tax-exempt cap HUF 32,280
  • Sick leave first 15 days: Employer pays 70%; after 15 days health insurance fund pays
  • Child Tax Exemption: Mothers with 4+ children lifetime exempt from personal income tax

Personal Income Tax (SZJA)

  • Flat rate 15% (Flat Tax): All income levels uniformly 15%; among the lowest in the EU
  • Youth under 25: Annual income not exceeding average gross wage exempt from personal income tax
  • Family tax base exemption: Per child can be exempt HUF 10,000 – 33,000 / month tax base (Increasing by number of children)

Overtime

  • Workday overtime: +50% wages or equivalent compensatory rest
  • Rest day overtime: +100% wages or equivalent compensatory rest
  • Statutory holiday overtime: +100% wages (Cannot be substituted with compensatory rest)
  • Night shift (22:00 – 06:00): +15% wages

Key Regulations

Labor Code (Munka Törvénykönyve, Mt.)

Effective 2012; regulating employment contracts, working hours, leave, dismissal protection, remote work and other core labor relations

Family-Friendly Tax Policy

4+ children mothers lifetime exempt from personal income tax; youth under 25 exempt from personal income tax; family tax base exemption can be allocated between spouses

SZÉP Leisure Card System

Common enterprise benefit; only 28% tax rate (Far lower than cash 46.5%); can be used for accommodation / dining / leisure

EU Labor Mobility

Hungary is an EU member state; EU / EEA citizens do not require work permits; third-country citizens must apply for work visa
