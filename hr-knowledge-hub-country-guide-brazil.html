Salary Benchmark

Brazil minimum wage (Salário Mínimo) nationally unified, announced by presidential executive order, 2025 R$1,518 / 2026 estimate R$1,580

  • Some states set regional minimum (Piso Regional, São Paulo / Rio / South) higher than federal standard
  • Mandatory 13 Salary (Décimo Terceiro), paid in two installments (November + December), applicable to all employees
  • 2025 full-time average monthly salary R$ 3,200, median R$ 2,800. São Paulo / Rio finance / tech significantly above national average
  • Manufacturing (Nissan / Volkswagen), Healthcare (HCA), Logistics (FedEx Memphis HQ) as pillar industries

Common Benefits

  • 13 Salary Décimo: Mandatory, November advance 50%, December 20th final payment
  • Vale Refeição / Alimentação (Meal Voucher): Common, monthly R$ 500-1,200, CLT benefit package
  • Vale Transporte (Transport Voucher): Mandatory provision, employee bears ≤ 6% salary portion
  • Private Medical Insurance (Plano de Saúde): Medium and large employer standard, monthly R$ 300-800 / person

CLT (Consolidação das Leis do Trabalho, 1943) is the labor law core. 2017 major reform (Reforma Trabalhista) introduced flexibility. Remains one of the world’s most comprehensive labor laws.

Termination Protection and Compensation

  • Without cause termination (Sem Justa Causa): 30 days advance notice (Aviso Prévio) + FGTS 40% penalty + remaining 13 Salary / vacation
  • With cause termination (Justa Causa): Serious misconduct, only remaining vacation + 13 Salary proportional
  • Mutual agreement termination (Acordo): 2017 introduced, advance notice / penalty / unemployment insurance reduced by half
  • Collective layoff (Demissão Coletiva): Requires union negotiation + judicial oversight

Key Regulations

CLT Consolidação das Leis do Trabalho (1943) — Labor Code

Brazil labor law core, 1943 enacted, 2017 modernization reform (Reforma Trabalhista), covering: working hours / wages / 13 Salary / termination compensation / FGTS. TST labor court final adjudication.

Reforma Tributária 2023 (EC 132) — Tax Reform

2023 Constitutional Amendment 132, merging ICMS+ISS+PIS+COFINS+IPI into IBS+CBS (Dual VAT), 2026-2033 gradual implementation, simplified 4-5 tier tax rate.

eSocial Digital Social Security Reporting System — Mandatory Full Compliance

Employers must submit real-time monthly reports on employees / wages / social security / work injury events to federal authorities. Multi-department data integration. Non-compliance subject to heavy penalties.

Lei de Cotas 8.213/91 — Disability / Apprentice Quota

100+ employee employers must reserve 2-5% positions for disabled persons, 5-15% apprentice quota. Labor Prosecution Office (MPT) oversight.

LGPD 2018 — General Data Protection Law

Similar to GDPR framework, employers collecting employee data must comply with explicit regulations. ANPD oversight. Maximum fine R$ 5,000 per violation / 2% of revenue.

NR-1 / SST Psychosocial Risk — 2025 New Regulations

2025 revised NR-1, employers must identify + control workplace psychosocial risks (Stress / Burnout). Aligned with EU standards.
