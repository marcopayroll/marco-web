Scope & Application

Scope and Legal Basis / 勿像基準法 · 所得稿法 · 健康保険法 · 历生年金保険法

This document applies to:

Companies that have established a local legal entity in Japan (栺式会社 KK / 合同会社 GK / 合資会社), with obtained 法人番号 (13-digit)
Employees directly hired by the local entity, subject to the Labour Standards Act (勿像基準法) and applicable employment rules (就業規則)
Payroll compliance based on Labour Standards Act, Income Tax Act, Health Insurance Act, Employees’ Pension Insurance Act, Employment Insurance Act, Workers’ Compensation Insurance Act, and regulations from 国稿床 (National Tax Agency) / 年金事务所 (Pension Office)

This framework covers how companies complete Payroll Set-up in Japan (registration at four offices: 稿务羚 tax office, 年金事务所 pension office, ハローワーク employment office, 勿基羚 labour standards office; マイナンバー employee identification), monthly salary calculation (withholding income tax, resident tax, social insurance), monthly filing, 年末調整 (year-end tax adjustment), and 退絍金 (severance settlement).

MARCO INSIGHT

Three key characteristics of Japanese Payroll: (1) Social insurance “three-piece set” + labour insurance “two-piece set” — Health Insurance / Employees’ Pension / Long-term Care Insurance managed by 年金事务所 (Pension Office) + 健康保険組合 (Health Insurance Association); Employment Insurance / Workers’ Compensation Insurance managed by ハローワーク (Employment Office) + 勿基羚 (Labour Standards Office); registration required at 4 separate offices; (2) Resident tax is not withheld and calculated by employer but notified by local government based on prior year income; employer must withhold monthly from June onwards of following year; (3) 年末調整 (Year-End Tax Adjustment) is a uniquely Japanese tax checkpoint — employer reconciles each employee’s full-year income tax in December and makes multi-payment or refunds.

MARCO INSIGHT

Three commonly underestimated details in Japanese Payroll: (1) Social insurance contribution base is fixed 标準報醬月额 (Standard Remuneration Monthly Amount) on 50/32-grade tables, updated once annually in July (定時決定); applied for full year — this causes timing lag when salary changes after premium determination; (2) 賞与 (bonuses) also incur social insurance, but Employees’ Pension bonus ceiling ¥1.5M per payment, Health Insurance annual combined ¥5.73M; (3) Resident tax is employee-exempt in first year (year of hire), withholding begins June of following year — foreign workers often experience “surprise” resident tax collection in second year; HR must provide advance notice.

MARCO INSIGHT

Three key changes to Japanese Payroll in 2026: (1) 2026 tax reform increases personal income tax exemption threshold from ¥1,030,000 to ¥1,780,000, with corresponding adjustments to spouse allowance and other deductions; (2) As of 2026/4, new “子ども・子育て拨出金” (Childcare Contribution) with employee share 0.115% + employer share 0.115%, mandatory for all employees, withheld together with health insurance; (3) Health insurance premium rates 2026/3 revision: Tokyo 9.91% → 9.85%, 40 prefectures nationwide reduced; Long-term Care 1.59% → 1.62%.
