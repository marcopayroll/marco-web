Scope & Application

Scope and Legal Basis / Employment Rights Act · HMRC · PAYE · NIC · Pensions Act

This document applies to:

Companies that have established a local legal entity in the UK (UK Ltd / PLC / LLP / UK Establishment Branch; minimum capital £1)
Employees directly hired by the local entity, subject to Employment Rights Act 1996, Equality Act 2010, and Working Time Regulations 1998
Payroll compliance based on ITEPA 2003, Social Security Contributions and Benefits Act, Pensions Act 2008, and regulations from HMRC / TPR / ACAS

This framework covers: how companies complete Payroll Set-up in the UK (Companies House registration + HMRC PAYE Scheme + Workplace Pension + Employers’ Liability Insurance), monthly salary calculation (PAYE income tax + employee/employer NIC + Auto-Enrolment pension + Apprenticeship Levy), RTI real-time filing per payroll + monthly PAYE/NIC payment (22nd of following month), and annual compliance management (P60 5/31, P11D 7/6, Self Assessment 1/31).

MARCO INSIGHT

Three key characteristics of UK Payroll: (1) RTI Real-Time Information — mandatory since 2013; every payroll must be submitted in real-time to HMRC via FPS (Full Payment Submission); (2) Employer NIC significant increase — as of 2025/4/6, rate increased from 13.8% to 15%, Secondary Threshold reduced from £9,100 to £5,000; expected to continue in 2026/27; employer employment costs significantly increased; (3) Auto-Enrolment mandatory — all employees aged 22 to statutory retirement age, annual salary ≥ £10,000 must be auto-enrolled into Workplace Pension (default NEST); combined contribution 8% (employer 3% + employee 5%).

MARCO INSIGHT

Three commonly underestimated details in UK Payroll: (1) PA Tapering — in £100k–£125,140 band, every additional £2 income reduces PA by £1, creating effective marginal tax rate of 60% (executive pay tax planning point); (2) Holiday Pay 52-week average salary method — since 2020 includes overtime / bonuses / commission; (3) IR35 / Employment Status — Off-Payroll Working Rules 2021 onwards means customer (employer) determines whether PSC contractor is substantively employed; non-compliance triggers direct PAYE + NIC + penalty recovery.

MARCO INSIGHT

Three key changes to UK Payroll in 2026: (1) Employer NIC elevated level — 15% / ST £5,000 frozen until 2030; permanent increase in employer employment cost ≈ £900/person/year (median salary); (2) National Living Wage 2026/4/1 increases +3.8% to £12.71/hour; (3) Employment Rights Bill — Unfair Dismissal protection changes from 2-year qualification to Day 1 hire; Sick Pay eliminates Waiting Days; Zero-hours contract new rules, etc.; expected to take effect progressively through 2026; HR must review policies.
