Mandatory Benefits

  • 13th Month Salary (Tet Bonus): Not legally mandatory, but virtually all enterprises provide 1 month salary as Lunar New Year bonus
  • Severance Pay: Per completed 1 year of service = half month salary (Normal contract termination)
  • Unemployment Allowance: Per completed 1 year of service = 1 month salary (Non-employee-caused termination)
  • Annual Leave Cash-out: Unused annual leave must be compensated in cash

Personal Income Tax (PIT)

  • Progressive 7 tiers (5% - 35%); employer monthly withholding; 2025 PIT Law amended, effective July 2026 with new tax rates
  • Exemption: Personal VND 11M / month + per dependent VND 4.4M / month (2026 H2 adjusted to 15.5M + 6.2M)

Dismissal Protection and Severance Pay

  • Employer-initiated dismissal: Must have statutory grounds + advance notice (Indefinite 45 days / Fixed 30 days / Seasonal 3 days)
  • Severance pay: Per completed 1 year of service = half month salary (Normal termination)
  • Unemployment allowance: Per completed 1 year = 1 month salary (Non-employee-caused termination, deducting years with unemployment insurance contributions)

Key Regulations

Labor Law 2019 (Bo Luat Lao Dong)

Effective January 2021; covering contracts, working hours, leave, dismissal protection, labor dispatch, foreign worker employment etc.

2025 PIT Law Amendment

Effective July 2026; 7 tiers changed to 5 tiers, exemption threshold raised to VND 15.5M / month, 35% tier starting point raised to VND 100M

Foreign Worker Work Permit & Union Fee

Must obtain Work Permit (Maximum 2 years renewable); employer must contribute 2% of wages to union fund (Even without a union)
